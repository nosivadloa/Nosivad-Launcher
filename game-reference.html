How to use these records

Each catalog can be filtered by name or term. These pages draw their base entries from the installed Legends of Aria: Eternal Knowledge Base. Nosivad's current skill and spell additions appear in the corresponding references, while the topical guides explain shard mechanics and tactics. The in-game windows remain the source for your character's learned spells, active quests, and current success chances.
