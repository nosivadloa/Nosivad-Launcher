Loot and Relics

Nosivad rewards scale with risk. Rarity, creature strength, rift status, breach grade, and boss status all influence what can drop.

How the Gear Roll Works

Nosivad gear uses a layered roll. First a creature rolls whether it drops gear at all. If it does, the item rolls its rarity, then the system rolls a gear grade and stat package. Harder enemies, stronger mutations, rifts, breaches, corruption, and bosses all improve the odds.

Dynamic Loot

Dynamic item rarity can roll from common through mythic. Better odds come from harder mobs, higher rarity enemies, rift and breach modifiers, and world boss kills. Some high-rarity rolls can convert into relic templates instead of ordinary gear.

Attunement Weapons

Mage Staffs and Novice's Wands are included in the enchanted-equipment pool, Breach Armament Caches, and boss-signature weapon selection. Both are magical weapon types used by Magical Attunement. Mage Profession I also awards a training staff, while advanced artifact staffs and wands remain schematic-based crafting rewards.

Graded Gear

Ranked gear uses the same E/D/C/B/A/S scale as players and Breach Gates. Higher grades add more stat lines and larger values, and the required Adventurer Grade must match before a player can equip that item.

Relics follow this rule too, including rings and necklaces. A relic's tooltip and name show its required grade. Grade unlocks occur at levels E/1, D/15, C/30, B/50, A/75, and S/95. An over-grade item remains yours but returns to your backpack when equipped.

Special Cases

Corrupted gear can roll stronger upside but also gains cursed negative stats. Boss signature gear rolls extra power and keeps the boss name as part of the item identity. Breach gear cannot roll above the grade of the breach it came from.

Arcane Recycler

The Arcane Recycler and Planar Forge stand beside the ordinary forge at the Eldeir smithy. Recycling permanently destroys selected Riftborn Gear and shows its guaranteed material yield before confirmation.

Every Riftborn salvage requires Channeling. Weapons and armor also require Blacksmithing and must be recycled within 6 meters of an Arcane Anvil. Jewelry, cloaks, instruments, and other Riftborn items require Fabrication plus a reusable Arcane Toolkit in the backpack. The Planar Forge assembles the toolkit for 10 Dust and 1 Shard; it is not consumed by recycling.

Profession Service Mastery

Use /professions to inspect all fourteen paths, current skill values, exact remaining requirements, trainer guidance, and available Start or Resume actions. Use /economy for wallet totals, merchant categories, recycler rules and yields, and directions to the Eldeir smithy, forge, and recycler area. Blacksmith ranks improve weapon and armor planar services; Tailor ranks improve Fabrication-class services for jewelry and other equipment.

Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, and Grandmaster add 1, 2, 3, or 4 Planar Dust to relevant salvage. Grandmasters also add 1 Shard when the item's base salvage already contains Shards. The same ranks reduce the Dust portion of relevant repair costs by 3%, 6%, 9%, or 12%. Essence and Core costs are never discounted, preserving high-end material sinks.

Connected Material Economy

Planar Rift completions award Planar Dust directly and add Shards at higher tiers. Recycling unwanted Riftborn Gear supplies larger Dust and Shard quantities plus high-grade Cores. Breach expeditions supply Breach Essence. Those combat rewards now feed repair, cleansing, affix work, grade improvement, relic awakening, Astral items, Planar Masterworks, and controlled player trade.

The server aggregates Planar material and major coin sources, sinks, and refunds by reason for live economy tuning. Native vendor prices and gathering yields remain unchanged during this measurement pass so later adjustments can be based on observed results instead of guesswork.

Planar Forge

The forge spends Planar Dust, Shards, Cores, and Breach Essence to repair damaged Riftborn Gear, replace one enchantment with a random compatible affix (three times per item), cleanse corruption and restore its recorded penalties, improve an item exactly one grade when it dropped below its source ceiling, or awaken one permanent trait on an A-grade or S-grade relic.

Repair cost scales with missing durability and grade. High-grade, corrupted, relic, and Masterwork repairs may require Breach Essence or a Core. Repairs require Channeling plus Blacksmithing for weapons and armor or Fabrication for jewelry and other equipment, preserving a service economy around skilled crafters.

Planar Masterworks

The Planar Forge can shape three account-bound A-grade Masterworks: a Riftsteel Armament, Veilbound Armor, or Astral Implement. Shaping requires 80 Channeling and 80 Blacksmithing for the weapon or armor, or 80 Fabrication plus an Arcane Toolkit for the implement. It costs 20 Dust, 3 Shards, and 3 Breach Essence.

Attunement costs 15 Dust, 5 Shards, and 2 Essence and chooses Ember (attack), Aegis (defense), Tempest (movement and stamina), Wellspring (mana and healing), or Fortune (better recycler returns). Reattunement costs 25 Dust, 6 Shards, 1 Core, and 4 Essence. Awakening requires 90 in both relevant skills and costs 30 Dust, 8 Shards, 1 Core, and 6 Essence. Exaltation requires 100 in both, costs 50 Dust, 12 Shards, 3 Cores, and 10 Essence, and is limited to one surviving Exalted Masterwork per character.

A Shaped or Attuned Masterwork can be released for trade for 8 Dust, 1 Shard, and 2 Essence. It becomes unclaimed and cannot be equipped or advanced until its buyer claims it at a Planar Forge. Awakened and Exalted Masterworks cannot change owners. Recycling still returns only a limited portion of invested materials.

Grade improvement is allowed only once, requires the destination Adventurer Grade, and can never exceed the source ceiling. Every forge operation displays its exact cost and asks for confirmation.

Worked Examples

A common wolf has a small chance to drop simple graded gear. A rare corrupted humanoid has much better odds because corruption, rarity, and category all help. A breach boss can drop high-grade breach gear plus additional rare rewards, and world bosses are even richer.

Breach Completion Caches

Armament Cache

Armament is the dependable gear option. It guarantees one random weapon or armor piece at the completed expedition's grade, with a matching grade requirement and randomized bonuses from that grade's power budget.

Planar Cache

Planar trades certainty for a higher-value roll. It grants additional Breach Essence and a grade-scaled chance at a random relic: E grants 1 Essence with a 3% relic chance, D grants 1 Essence with a 7% chance, and C grants 2 Essence with an 18% chance. Completing the current C-grade optional challenge improves this to 3 Essence and a 30% relic chance.

Both choices also include the expedition's normal base gold and Essence. The gatekeeper's corpse loot is separate, so choosing Armament or Planar does not replace the boss's guaranteed graded-equipment drop. See Breach Gates for the full cache table and Cartographer option.
