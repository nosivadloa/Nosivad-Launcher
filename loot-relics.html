Loot and Relics

Nosivad rewards scale with risk. Rarity, creature strength, rift status, breach grade, and boss status all influence what can drop.

How the Gear Roll Works

Nosivad gear uses a layered roll. First a creature rolls whether it drops gear at all. If it does, the item rolls its rarity, then the system rolls a gear grade and stat package. Harder enemies, stronger mutations, rifts, breaches, corruption, and bosses all improve the odds.

Dynamic Loot

Dynamic item rarity can roll from common through mythic. Better odds come from harder mobs, higher rarity enemies, rift and breach modifiers, and world boss kills. Some high-rarity rolls can convert into relic templates instead of ordinary gear.

Attunement Weapons

Mage Staffs and Novice's Wands are included in the enchanted-equipment pool, Breach Armament Caches, and boss-signature weapon selection. Both are magical weapon types used by Magical Attunement. Mage Profession I also awards a training staff, while advanced artifact staffs and wands remain schematic-based crafting rewards.

Graded Gear

Ranked gear uses the same E/D/C/B/A/S scale as players and Breach Gates. Higher grades add more stat lines and larger values, and the required Adventurer Grade must match before a player can equip that item.

Special Cases

Corrupted gear can roll stronger upside but also gains cursed negative stats. Boss signature gear rolls extra power and keeps the boss name as part of the item identity. Breach gear cannot roll above the grade of the breach it came from.

Worked Examples

A common wolf has a small chance to drop simple graded gear. A rare corrupted humanoid has much better odds because corruption, rarity, and category all help. A breach boss can drop high-grade breach gear plus additional rare rewards, and world bosses are even richer.

Breach Completion Caches

Armament Cache

Armament is the dependable gear option. It guarantees one random weapon or armor piece at the completed expedition's grade, with a matching grade requirement and randomized bonuses from that grade's power budget.

Planar Cache

Planar trades certainty for a higher-value roll. It grants additional Breach Essence and a grade-scaled chance at a random relic: E grants 1 Essence with a 3% relic chance, D grants 1 Essence with a 7% chance, and C grants 2 Essence with an 18% chance. Completing the current C-grade optional challenge improves this to 3 Essence and a 30% relic chance.

Both choices also include the expedition's normal base gold and Essence. The gatekeeper's corpse loot is separate, so choosing Armament or Planar does not replace the boss's guaranteed graded-equipment drop. See Breach Gates for the full cache table and Cartographer option.
