Vocation Renown

A practical guide to gathering and crafting progression

Start Here

Type /vocation or /tradelevel. Gather resources, make useful items, or complete gathering missions and crafting orders. These activities build one character-specific Vocation Level from 1 to 100.

Vocation is separate from Adventurer Level, individual skills, profession quest ranks, and regional Renown. It does not raise your combat stats or replace skill training.

Open Trade Insights when you have a point to spend. Select Gathering or Crafting and read the requirements before choosing. Open Commissions for goals unlocked by your learned Insights.

Example: mining raises Gathering contribution and your shared Vocation XP. Crafting a useful recipe raises Crafting contribution and the same shared level. You may develop both paths.

What the Numbers Mean

Level is your overall Vocation progression. XP Progress shows progress within the current level, not your lifetime total. Rank is the public name associated with your level.

Gathering and Crafting contribution record how much Vocation XP came from each category. They are requirements for that category's Insights, not separate levels or spendable currencies.

Trade Insights are spendable points: one is earned every five levels, for 20 points by level 100. Each listed Insight costs one point and also checks level, category contribution, and any prerequisite.

Ranks

Level 1: Laborer; 15: Tradesperson; 30: Artisan; 50: Specialist; 75: Master Artisan; 95: Luminary; 100: Guild Laureate.

Your numeric XP and contribution are private. Vocation Rank and voluntarily equipped titles provide public recognition. Previous eligible profession progress may establish a one-time starting level.

Earning XP

Successful Mining, Lumberjacking, Fishing, and supported cotton/textile collection contribute Gathering XP. Bonus resource quantity does not multiply the award. Skinning, Digging, buying, selling, and township donations do not count as ordinary gathering.

Successful ordinary crafts can contribute Crafting XP. Recipe difficulty matters: outleveled recipes award less. Basic recipes requiring skill 0 remain eligible; negative-requirement utility recipes, explicitly excluded recipes, and artifact schematics are excluded. A failed craft can still train its native skill, but does not earn successful-craft Vocation XP.

Gathering mission settlement, crafting-order hand-ins, and eligible crafting/gathering profession-tier completion also award XP. Eligible equipment repairs award XP after completion, with a six-hour repeat-award cooldown on that item.

Use XP: On or XP: Off in /vocation to control the Vocation feedback messages. Turning feedback off does not turn progression off.

Ordinary-craft base XP is 2 below requirement 30, 4 at 30-49, 7 at 50-79, 10 at 80-99, and 14 at 100+. More than 20 skill above the requirement halves the award; more than 40 quarters it, rounded down with a minimum of 1 XP.

Insight Requirements

The earliest Gathering and Crafting Insights require level 5 and 500 contribution in their own category.

Level-30 choices require 10,500 category contribution; level-50 choices require 43,500; level-75 choices require 122,500; level-95 choices require 277,500.

A high shared level alone is insufficient. A character who leveled mostly through crafting must build Gathering contribution before learning advanced Gathering Insights. Tier-II upgrades also require their Tier-I Insight.

Gathering Choices

Tempered Tools I/II reduce eligible Mining, Lumberjacking, and Fishing tool wear by 5%/10%. Gatherer's Rhythm I/II conserve 3%/6% stamina over time; small costs are accumulated rather than visibly discounted on every action.

Surveyor unlocks regional surveys. Field Notes unlocks your Gathering Journal. Regional Specialist adds one mission choice when the dispatcher can provide it.

Survey Portfolio permits two unfinished surveys; Advanced Regional Surveys unlocks the larger survey. Master's Implements unlocks a ceremonial cosmetic claim. Luminary Surveyor unlocks the advanced gathering commission.

Crafting Choices

Careful Repair I/II builds 3%/6% ordinary-repair material credit over time. Repairs still cost at least one material. Workshop Ledger shows crafts, repairs, and orders recorded after its tracking became active.

Order Appraisal reveals order Vocation XP. Commission Choice adds one option to newly generated crafting-order lists. Commission Reroll permits one free replacement of an unaccepted list per UTC day.

Maker's Signature is an optional cosmetic mark on eligible newly crafted non-stacked items. Master Commission unlocks regional production goals, Laureate Workshop a cosmetic workstation claim, and Luminary Commission the advanced production goal.

Surveys and Commissions

Basic survey: 60 eligible successful gathering actions in the acceptance region, for 500 XP. Advanced survey: 100 actions, for 1,000 XP. Travel to a supported outdoor region before accepting its survey.

Regional Production: 25 ordinary crafts and one crafting-order hand-in in the acceptance region, for 1,000 XP.

Luminary Gathering: 150 actions covering Mining, Lumberjacking, and Fishing across at least three supported regions, for 2,000 XP. Luminary Production: 50 crafts, three repairs, and three order hand-ins, for 2,000 XP.

Acceptance begins a rolling seven-day issue cooldown. Survey cooldowns are regional; commission types have their own slots. Abandoning or resetting Insights does not erase cooldowns. The window shows the exact current objectives and availability.

Changing Your Build and Claiming Rewards

Resetting Insights costs 1,000 gold plus 250 gold per spent Insight, with a 24-hour cooldown. For example, resetting four spent points costs 2,000 gold. Your Vocation XP and level are retained.

Resetting cancels active survey/commission progress associated with the old build; cooldowns and previously claimed rewards remain. Review the confirmation before paying.

Cosmetic implement, workstation, and capstone chronicle claims are character-bound and one-time. Level 100 unlocks the selectable Guild Laureate title.

Common Questions

Why is an Insight locked? Check all three requirements: shared level, contribution in its category, and prerequisite. An available Insight point does not bypass them.

Why did I get no craft XP? Check recipe difficulty and whether the craft succeeded. Basic skill-0 recipes remain eligible, while negative-requirement utility recipes and excluded schematics do not provide ordinary-craft XP.

Vocation does not currently increase ordinary resource yields or production output. The extra-resource economic perk is disabled. Utility benefits, access, and cosmetic rewards are the current focus.

A missing XP message alone does not prove an award failed: feedback can be disabled and messages are aggregated. Compare the XP and contribution shown in /vocation. Report persistent collection or navigation problems with /bug.

What Is a Regional Survey?

A regional survey is a gathering assignment you accept through /vocation > Trade Insights > Gathering Journal. Learn Surveyor first, travel to a supported outdoor region, and press its Basic survey button.

Complete 60 successful eligible gathering actions in the named region for 500 Vocation XP. An active or unfinished survey is simply one you accepted but have not completed.

Survey Portfolio changes your capacity from one active survey to two. For example, accept one in Upper Plains and another in Eastern Frontier, then work on each as you travel. The actions and rewards for each assignment stay the same.

What Each Trade Insight Gives You

Tempered Tools I: Mining, Lumberjacking, and Fishing tools wear out 5% more slowly, so they last longer before repair.

Gatherer's Rhythm I: Spend 3% less stamina over time while Mining, Lumberjacking, or Fishing. Savings build across actions, rather than on every action.

Surveyor: Unlock gathering assignments in the Gathering Journal. A basic survey requires 60 successful actions in its region and pays 500 Vocation XP.

Field Notes: Open Gathering Journal to see your successful gathering totals by activity, region, and resource. This is a personal record, not an extra-resource bonus.

Tempered Tools II: Improve tool-wear savings from 5% to 10% for Mining, Lumberjacking, and Fishing. This replaces the first rank; it does not add another 10%.

Gatherer's Rhythm II: Improve gathering stamina savings from 3% to 6% over time. This replaces the first rank; it does not increase harvesting speed.

Regional Specialist: Gathering dispatchers can offer one extra mission choice. Choose from more assignments when a suitable additional mission is available.

Survey Portfolio: Keep two gathering surveys active instead of one. Accept a survey in each region and work toward both XP rewards as you travel. Requires Surveyor.

Advanced Regional Surveys: Accept advanced surveys in Gathering Journal: complete 100 successful gathering actions in the assigned region for 1,000 Vocation XP.

Master's Implements: Claim a character-bound ceremonial gathering implement from Commissions & Rewards. It is cosmetic; it does not improve gathering speed or yield.

Luminary Surveyor: Unlock a gathering commission: 150 actions covering Mining, Lumberjacking, and Fishing across at least three regions, for 2,000 Vocation XP.

Careful Repair I: Save 3% of ordinary Repair Gear materials over time. Small savings build into credit for later repairs; each repair still costs at least one material.

Workshop Ledger: See your recorded crafts, repairs, and crafting-order completions in Trade Insights. This tracks activity; it does not increase craft success.

Careful Repair II: Improve ordinary repair material savings from 3% to 6% over time. This replaces the first rank and keeps the one-material minimum.

Order Appraisal: See how much Vocation XP an offered crafting order will award before accepting it. This reveals its payout; it does not increase the reward.

Commission Choice: New crafting-order lists can include one extra order to choose from. It expands your choice, not the rewards from each completed order.

Maker's Signature: Use Commissions & Rewards to turn your cosmetic signature on or off. Eligible newly crafted non-stacked items show your name and Vocation rank.

Commission Reroll: Replace an unaccepted crafting-order list once per UTC day for free. Use the order giver's refresh option to look for a different selection.

Master Commission: Unlock a regional production assignment: complete 25 ordinary crafts and one crafting-order hand-in in its region for 1,000 Vocation XP.

Laureate Workshop: Claim a character-bound decorative workstation from Commissions & Rewards. It is a cosmetic reward, not a working crafting station.

Luminary Commission: Unlock a production assignment: complete 50 ordinary crafts, three repairs, and three crafting-order hand-ins for 2,000 Vocation XP.

Direct Bonuses and Assignment Unlocks

Some Insights make everyday actions cheaper: Tempered Tools reduces wear, Gatherer's Rhythm conserves stamina, and Careful Repair saves materials over time. Other Insights unlock assignments, show activity records, offer more choices, or provide cosmetics.

Vocation currently grants no Strength, harvesting-speed, or harvest-yield bonus. An assignment reward is additional Vocation XP, not an increase to the resources you gather.
