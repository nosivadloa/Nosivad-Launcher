Combat Stats (29)

The official descriptions of combat stats and their effects.

These entries explain what the stat numbers mean. Nosivad changes some caps and bonuses; see Skills and Stats for shard rules.

Will

Description: Mental fortitude and determination. Affects resistance to mental effects and spell concentration. Will increases resistance to dismount effects.

Category: Mental

Influences: Mental resistance, Spell concentration, Bloodlust pool (MaxBloodlust stat), Vitality regeneration

Agility

Description: Speed, dexterity, and reflexes. Affects attack speed, movement speed, and evasion.

Category: Physical

Influences: Attack speed, Movement speed, Evasion chance, Accuracy, Stamina pool (MaxStamina stat)

Wisdom

Description: Spiritual awareness and mana efficiency. Affects mana regeneration and spell resistance.

Category: Mental

Influences: Mana efficiency, Mana regeneration (ManaRegen stat), Spell resistance, Mana pool (MaxMana stat)

Strength

Description: Physical power and raw damage potential. Affects melee damage, carrying capacity, and some weapon requirements.

Category: Physical

Influences: Melee damage (Attack stat), Carrying capacity (MaxWeight stat), Health points (MaxHealth stat), Weapon requirements

Intelligence

Description: Magical aptitude and spell power. Affects mana pool, spell damage, and magical abilities.

Category: Mental

Influences: Magical damage (Power stat), Mana pool (MaxMana stat), Spell effectiveness, Mana regeneration

Max Bloodlust

Description: Maximum bloodlust for martial abilities.

Category: Resources

Evasion

Description: Ability to avoid incoming attacks.

Category: Combat

Bloodlust Regen

Description: Rate of bloodlust recovery over time.

Category: Regeneration

Max Mana

Description: Maximum mana points for casting spells.

Category: Resources

Attack Speed

Description: Seconds between attacks. Lower is faster.

Category: Combat

Force

Description: Raw physical force for special attacks.

Category: Combat

Health Regen

Description: Rate of health point recovery over time.

Category: Regeneration

Defense

Description: Physical damage resistance and armor effectiveness.

Category: Combat

Power

Description: Magical damage and spell effectiveness.

Category: Combat

Max Health

Description: Maximum health points.

Category: Resources

Accuracy

Description: Chance to successfully hit targets.

Category: Combat

Mount Speed

Description: How quickly you move while mounted.

Category: Movement

Attack

Description: Melee damage potential with weapons.

Category: Combat

Move Speed

Description: How quickly you move on foot.

Category: Movement

Stamina Regen

Description: Rate of stamina recovery over time.

Category: Regeneration

Mana Regen

Description: Rate of mana point recovery over time.

Category: Regeneration

Max Weight

Description: Maximum carrying capacity.

Category: Resources

Max Stamina

Description: Maximum stamina for physical activities.

Category: Resources
