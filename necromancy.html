Necromancy and the Necromancer

Study grave-born spellcraft, inscribe a bone-and-blood grimoire, and call temporary skeleton servants to your side.

Start the Necromancer Profession

  1. Train Necromancy to 30.
  2. Speak with a mage trainer to begin [Necromancer I] The First Binding.
  3. Craft a Necronomicon through Inscription and return to the trainer.
  4. At 50 Necromancy, after completing Tier I, speak with a mage trainer to begin [Necromancer II] The Warder's Folio. Craft a Raise Skeleton scroll and return to receive the Bone Ward scroll recipe.

Necromancer quest markers can lead you to mage trainers in Barren Lands, Eldeir, or Valus.

Craft Your Necronomicon

Make the Necronomicon with Inscription 35 using 10 Frayed Scrolls, 3 Blood, and 3 Bones. It works as a spellbook: place a Necromancy scroll on the book to learn its spell. The scroll is consumed when it is added.

Necromancy Spells

Craft these scrolls through Inscription, then learn them in your Necronomicon. The listed value is the minimum Inscription skill to craft each scroll.

SpellInscriptionWhat it does
Blight15Afflicts an enemy with grave-blight.
Curse25Weakens your target.
Grave Mend35Restores health.
Bone Ward35; recipe from Tier IIBriefly improves your defense.
Raise Skeleton45Summons a temporary skeleton servant.
Raise Skeleton Archer60Summons a temporary ranged skeleton.
Raise Skeleton Mage75Summons a temporary skeleton mage that attacks with lightning.

Command Your Summons

You may have up to two Necromancy summons at a time. Any combination of skeleton servants counts toward this shared limit. Summons are temporary, so choose when to call them into a fight.
