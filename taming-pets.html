Taming and Pets

Raise a companion from level 1 to 100, choose creature-themed combat abilities, and carry stored pets in a Tamer's Grimoire.

Managing Your Pets

Use /pet to open Taming & Pet Management. The scrolling window shows Animal Taming, Animal Lore, Beastmastery, active control slots, Grimoire volume, and stable count. Each active pet shows health, level, XP toward its next level, and its current bonuses. You can order pets to Follow, Stay, or Stop; change combat stance; attack or tame a target; and review learned abilities. Stable pets remain visible in the list, but retrieval still belongs to a stable master.

Leveling and Abilities

Newly tamed pets begin at level 1. Pets earn variable XP from eligible enemies they help defeat; stronger encounters can grant more, with a per-kill cap. Levels increase maximum health, attack, and defense on a curve, with further growth through level 100. Pet progress survives ordinary stabling, Grimoire storage, death, and server restarts.

At levels 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100, the owner chooses one of three abilities suited to that creature's family. Choosing the same ability at a later milestone improves its rank. Learned abilities trigger automatically during eligible combat and use effects themed to the creature. The ability window in /pet also lets you review choices.

Releasing a pet is permanent for its Nosivad progression: its earned level, XP, bonuses, and learned abilities are removed as it returns to a base creature.

Tamer's Grimoire

Craft a Tamer's Grimoire with 50 Inscription using six Blank Scrolls, two Boards, and one Leather, or buy one from a scribe for 750 gold. The first use binds it to your character; only one bound Grimoire can be used per character.

The Grimoire holds 10 volume. A pet uses volume equal to its control-slot size, so larger creatures occupy more space. Stored pets keep their identity and progress without using active control or ordinary stable slots. From /pet, select Store in Grimoire for an eligible nearby pet, or Summon beside a stored pet when active control slots permit.

  • Storing requires a living nearby pet you control, no combat, an empty pet backpack, and enough free Grimoire volume. Dismount before storing a mount.
  • Summoning requires enough active pet slots and cannot be done while you are in combat.
  • Temporary or summoned pets cannot be booked. A stable master does not retrieve Grimoire pets; use /pet.

Creature Size

The active limit remains five control slots. Grimoire capacity is separate. A Warg uses four slots and four Grimoire volume; smaller creatures vary by their authored control size. The /pet window shows each pet's actual slot and volume cost before you store it.
