Enchanting

How material capacity, essence levels, and difficulty shape base enchantment attempts.

The base enchantment system permits up to 3 enchants per item and 3 essences per attempt. The base copper charge is 300. Material limits decide how much enchantment capacity a crafted item can support.

Difficulty and costs

The official difficulty formula is (slots_used * 5) + (sum of essence_level^2) * 2. Enchantment levels use slot costs of {'1': 1, '2': 2, '3': 3}. Higher level essences increase difficulty quickly. Check the in-game enchantment window before committing materials.

Nosivad also has planar equipment and Forge actions that work differently from base enchanting; see Loot and Relics.
