Nosivad's latest server update · October 25, 2026

Legends of Aria Eternal 1.0.6.0

Nosivad has updated its server content for Legends of Aria Eternal 1.0.6.0 and client 1.5.0.

Welcome to the Fallen Realm

The native Hell region is presented in Nosivad as the Fallen Realm. Its gate keeps the native opening schedule, while the region retains Nosivad's PvE protections.

Planar Marks

Planar Marks can be exchanged for planar materials and items. Marks previously known as Breach Sigils remain usable; they are no longer keys for entering the gate.

Keep exploring

Open the Rift Breach guide for travel information, or browse the Knowledge Base for Nosivad's systems and activities.

The separate NosivadRift region is not part of this server update.
