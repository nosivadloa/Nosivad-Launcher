Regional Identity And Events

Nosivad's regions have distinctive enemy families, contracts, resources, collectibles, named bosses, rewards, and rotating local events. Traveling to another town should mean finding a different gameplay loop—not merely another copy of the same board.

Use /events anywhere to see each active event's archetype, objective, stage progress, location, remaining time, credited responders, and enemies defeated. Every event card has a personal Track button.

How Events Work

  • One regional event runs shard-wide at a time; waiting regions retry when the shared schedule permits.
  • Each region rotates its own local event pool and avoids immediately repeating the same event.
  • Events can use combat, movement, interaction, defense, roaming, or staged objectives rather than always ending at one stationary boss.
  • Event-specific enemies and resource nodes can provide their town's recovery materials.
  • Players credited on at least two enemy defeats, two objective steps, or the boss receive a completion participation reward.
  • Completed events return sooner when players are actively responding. Ignored or expired events receive a longer recovery period.
  • Event-boss corpses remain for normal looting and decay.

Exploration Circuits

Each town board now offers a three-stop exploration contract. Waypoints record automatically when you reach them, including town squares, roads, camps, coasts, ruins, and event sites. These circuits provide coins, a town-specific reward, and regional renown when reported to the local leader.

Finding Active Events

/events is the authoritative player view. In addition to locations and timers, it reports current responder count and enemy defeats so late arrivals can judge whether an event still needs help. Use /events alerts on for one notice per new event run or /events alerts off to silence those notices.

Travel And Regional Progress

Town boards, leaders, exploration circuits, local resources, rotating bosses, and event sites now form distinct regional loops. Use /adventure for current contract direction and /renown for long-term regional standing.
