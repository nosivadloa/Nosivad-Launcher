Regional Identity And Events

Nosivad's regions have distinctive enemy families, contracts, resources, collectibles, named bosses, rewards, and rotating local events. Traveling to another town should mean finding a different gameplay loop—not merely another copy of the same board.

Use /events anywhere to see every active regional event, its region, coordinates, named boss, and remaining time.

How Events Work

  • Each overworld region schedules its own event cycle. Southern Hills alternates the Valus and Belhaven events.
  • Events populate their site with regional enemies and a named boss.
  • Local event enemies can advance matching contracts and supply regional materials.
  • Defeating the named boss completes the event. Unfinished events disperse when their active timer expires.
  • Event-boss corpses remain after death for normal corpse looting and decay.

Finding Active Events

/events is the authoritative player view. It reports active event names, regions, coordinates, bosses, and remaining time, so players can decide where to travel without guessing which region's timer fired.

Travel And Regional Progress

Adventurer destinations now extend beyond the original Eldeir, Helm, and Pyros hubs. Town boards, leaders, renown rewards, local resources, and event sites encourage longer routes across the overworld. Use /adventure for current contract direction and /renown for long-term regional standing.
