Housing, Storage and Mounts

A practical guide to the account-wide starter-home rule, protected house containers, container trading, and Nosivad horse armor.

Starter Home

The Carpenter's starter-home path follows the native one-plot-per-account rule. The quest checks the account's remaining plot capacity before offering or completing the deed step, so an alternate character is not sent to buy a plot that the account cannot place.

If the account already owns a plot, an incomplete starter-home record may be cleared during login repair. Completed quest history and player items are preserved.

Secure House Storage

Locked-down secure chests may be arranged vertically with Move Object. If nearby house construction or decoration blocks the ordinary sight ray, an authorized player within normal interaction range can still open the top-level chest, move contained items, and split contained stacks.

  • Normal house ownership, co-owner, friend, and container-access settings still apply.
  • Locks, range, trade, bank, merchant, noloot, weight, and capacity rules remain enforced.
  • Ordinary containers, nested containers, remote chests, and secure containers the player does not control receive no exception.

Trading Containers

Empty containers can be traded normally. A pouch, bag, or other container that still holds items is returned intact to its owner's backpack before trade acceptance. Empty a container before trading it; this prevents nested contents from entering the unsafe native transfer path and does not create replacement items.

Horse Armor Kits

Eldeir, Helm, Valus, Pyros Township, and Tethys renown rewards use movable Horse Armor Kits. Keep the kit in your backpack, choose Apply Horse Armor, target your own living and unsaddled horse within three units, then confirm.

  • The process consumes the horse and kit and creates an armored horse statue that can be summoned as a mount.
  • The original horse type and color are retained.
  • Already saddled, armored, packed, dismissing, equipped, dead, distant, or unowned horses are rejected; other animal species are not eligible.
  • A Horse Armor Kit does not upgrade an existing mount statue.

Mounts Indoors and at Pyros Docks

Mounted players are dismounted in authored interiors and roof-bound player houses. The outdoor Pyros fish dock and its approaches remain rideable even where an overlapping House12 map region would otherwise look like an interior. Other Pyros buildings and player houses still use normal indoor dismount rules.
