Magic Schools, Skills, and Spells

For individual spell effects, circles, and costs, use the complete spell reference. For casting and support skills, see all skills.

Six paths of magic share a spellbook interface but ask different things of a caster. Each specialist school uses its own casting skill. Browse by school and filter Known or Missing spells in the spellbook; learn new spells from matching inscription scrolls.

The lore below follows the schools' trainer dialogue and spell themes. Spell effects and progression describe the current shard definitions; the spellbook shows the current circle, mana cost, and tooltip for your character.

Magery — the familiar arcane craft

Tradition. Magery is the broad, established practice from which many players know utility, travel, and combat magic. Its Mark, Recall, and Portal make it the reference point for the newer schools' travel rites.

Skill and use. Magery spells use Magery. Channeling supports Focus and mana recovery across magical builds; Magic Affinity appears in mage profession training. A spell with a familiar name can have a separate specialist version, so check the skill in the spellbook.

Spells. Mark records an eligible rune destination, Recall returns you to a marked destination, and Portal opens group travel. Magery remains useful when a build values general utility alongside a specialized combat school.

Evocation — shape the elements

Tradition. Caldra Ashmantle teaches that raw force answers quickly and punishes carelessness. The Evocator trials move from a measured spark to battlefield fire and finally lightning. The lesson is control of where damage lands, not simply greater force.

Skill and path. Evocation casts use Evocation skill. Evocator certification has skill milestones at 30, 50, 80, and 100 with sequential trainer trials; the trainer supplies scrolls as the path advances.

  • Cinderbolt is a quick single-target fire cast. Glacial Spear damages and slows one foe. Storm Lance is focused lightning, while Thunderclap is a heavier strike.
  • Pillar of Fire hits a chosen location, Flamewave advances through an area, and Ice Rain pressures a targeted area over time. Positioning matters when fighting around allies or terrain.
  • Elemental Rupture rewards setup: burning, chill, or shock already on the target triggers extra damage.
  • Cinderlight supplies light; Summon Ember Steed calls a temporary mount.

Warding — keep the line intact

Tradition. Ser Talia Vey describes a ward as a promise that someone will endure. Warder trials progress from guarding one ally to sheltering a company, adding restoration to defense. This school suits a caster who watches the whole fight and intervenes before a collapse.

Skill and path. Warding casts use Warding skill. Warder tiers follow the 30, 50, 80, and 100 skill milestones and trainer quests.

  • Aegis and Bulwark raise defense; Repel returns a limited amount of incoming spell damage. A brief anti-interruption ward helps an ally finish casting under pressure.
  • Purifying Ward cures poison and adds short protection. Circle of Purity cleanses nearby allies.
  • Heal and Greater Heal restore one ally, while Circle of Mending restores nearby allies. Sanctuary's Grace is a high-circle heal over time; Resurrect revives a fallen ally.
  • Warden's Rebuke gives the school a direct force attack. Warden's Light illuminates; Call Guardian Charger provides a temporary mount.

Conjuration — bind with purpose

Tradition. Orien Vell begins with precision and restraint. His trials teach that a summoned ally must be guided and a compact maintained; even the Rift Colossus is presented as a test of control. Conjuration's identity is an active relationship with temporary bound forces.

Skill and path. Conjuration casts use Conjuration skill. Conjurer tiers follow 30, 50, 80, and 100 skill milestones with scroll rewards.

  • Arcane Dart is a low-circle force bolt. Aetheric Snare deals modest aether damage and briefly slows a foe. Black Hole pulls hostile creatures inward and damages those near its center.
  • Cinder Sentinel and Rift Colossus fill guardian roles; Frostbound Hound is a striker; Arcane Wisp and Ashen Acolyte are casters. Energy Vortex and Arcane Storm are location summons.
  • Bound Aegis increases active summon defense, Rally the Bound raises their attack speed, Mend the Bound heals injured Conjuration summons, and Recall the Bound returns them to your side.
  • Conjured Beacon gives light; Call Astral Steed is a temporary mount.

Magical summons share a five-summon and five-slot limit. Stronger allies use more slots; the Rift Colossus uses all five. Conjuration summons also count against pet capacity.

Verdancy — answer the living world

Tradition. Aldren Thornweave tells new Druids that nature answers those who listen. The trials move from light and careful offense to healing, a hunter's aspect, and the burden of old growth. Verdancy balances harm, care, and temporary borrowed forms.

Skill and path. Verdancy casts use Verdancy skill. Druid tiers use the same 30, 50, 80, and 100 milestones and trainer trials; the Verdant Codex is the school's book.

  • Briar Dart offers focused offense and Bramble Snare slows a foe. Virulent Bloom applies poison; Briarstorm pressures an area.
  • Verdant Mend restores one ally, Purify removes poison, Circle of Spring heals nearby allies, and Barkskin adds protection.
  • Wolf, Bear, Grove Stalker, Dire Wolf, Great Bear, and Primal Avatar are temporary beast aspects. Only one aspect remains active at a time; choose it for the fight ahead.
  • Summon Spirit Wolf, Call Sproutling, and Call Ancient Treant call temporary nature allies. Verdant Light illuminates and Call Verdant Bear supplies a temporary mount.

Verdancy summons share magical summon and pet capacity with the other schools.

Necromancy — command without surrender

Tradition. Mordain's instruction warns that the dead are not tools to handle carelessly. Necromancer trials take the student from a blood-and-bone grimoire through restless places, Contempt, and the Catacombs. The final lesson is to return with one's will intact and make servants serve a purpose.

Skill and path. Necromancy spells use Necromancy skill. The Necromancer profession progresses through trainer quests and the 30, 50, 80, and 100 skill milestones. The Necronomicon is its spellbook; Inscription can craft one from frayed scrolls, blood, and bones. A scroll is consumed when learned.

  • Animate Flesh, Animate Bones, and Extract Spirit target suitable corpses to create temporary servants. The source corpse's E–S grade changes the resulting variant and may raise its slot cost. These rites replaced the old Raise Skeleton family.
  • Soul Siphon steals vitality; Frailty weakens a foe; Corpse Explosion turns a corpse into an attack; Gravefire scorches an area with grave energy.
  • Life Tap and Soul Transfusion trade or move vitality; Bone Ward adds brief defense; Gravebound Renewal heals injured Necromancy summons. Reaper's Covenant is a high-circle finisher that spends more than mana.
  • Graveglow gives light and Raise Grave Steed calls a temporary mount.

Travel across the schools

Each specialist school has its own Mark, Recall, and short step: Evocation has Ember Mark, Ashen Recall, and Flashstep; Warding has Warden's Mark, Sanctuary Recall, and Aegis Step; Conjuration has Rift Anchor, Wayfarer's Recall, and Riftstep; Verdancy has Verdant Mark, Roots Return, and Wildstep; Necromancy has Grave Mark, Death's Recall, and Grave Step. Keep a rune in your backpack and mark only at an eligible location. Valid static, buried, and outlaw-only runes remain protected from casual remarking.
