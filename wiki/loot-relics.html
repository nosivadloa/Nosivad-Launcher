Loot and Relics

Nosivad rewards scale with risk. Enemy rank, strength, mutation, rift difficulty, corruption, and boss status all influence what can drop.

Dynamic Loot

Eligible non-animal enemies can roll enchanted equipment from common through mythic. Better odds and stronger bonuses come from higher ranks, mutations, Planar Rift enemies, corrupted hosts, and bosses.

Planar Crafting Economy

Recycle eligible Rift and Breach equipment into planar materials. Breach Essence joins Dust, Shards, and Cores in the upgrade economy, while corruption can improve an item's power at a cost. Skilled professions receive rank-based salvage and repair benefits. Astral and other relic equipment, Masterwork crafting, and player trade connect these materials to the wider shard economy.

Graded Enchanted Gear

Enchanted gear uses the same E/D/C/B/A/S scale as players and ranked enemies. An item cannot roll above the grade of the enemy or Planar Rift that produced it, and players can only equip enchanted gear at or below their own grade.
