Town Renown

Earn separate standing with regional leaders.

Ranks

Renown is recorded separately for 15 towns, regions, and expeditions. Rank thresholds are 0, 25, 60, 110, and 180 points. Leaders offer commissions and can record eligible completed local service once. Use /renown to see each rank, next threshold, reward, and earned capstone perk.

Rewards

Each new rank grants a practical regional reward, and the final rank gives a permanent passive benefit. Eldeir's progression includes field supplies, a wayfarer kit, horse armor, and a final skill-gain perk. Other regions have their own rewards. The in-game renown window is authoritative for your character's current claim state.
