Regional Events

Dynamic objectives give every region its own emergencies, rewards, and reasons to travel.

Event archetypes

  • Regional invasion: repel hostile forces threatening a settlement or road.
  • Escort: protect a traveler through a sequence of dangerous waypoints.
  • Resource surge: stabilize corrupted resources while enemies contest the site.
  • Shrine defense: hold sacred ground against waves of attackers.
  • Roaming elite: track a powerful patrol moving through the region.
  • Multi-stage rift: complete linked objectives before confronting the final planar threat.

Up to two regions can host active regional events shard-wide at the same time. Use /events to see current locations, objective progress, and stage information.
