Breach Gates

An active Breach Gate admits players to a graded Fallen Realm expedition. Bring the matching Planar Mark in your backpack; the gate consumes one when admission succeeds.

Grades

GradeDifficulty Intent
ENovice expedition for learning the route.
DEarly dungeon practice.
CMid-tier breach dungeon.
BDangerous group content.
AHigh-end group content.
SRare brutal endgame content.

Expeditions

Authored grade routes include E-grade Moonroot Hunting Ground, D-grade Emberchain Warcamp, C-grade Crypt of the Severed Veil, B-grade Astral Observatory, A-grade Glass Wastes, and S-grade Unbound Convergence. Each has rooms, waves, and a gatekeeper; higher grades allow more time and increase the danger. An old gate marked Retired is not an active entrance.

Rooms and challenges

Clear each wave and its room objective before the expedition stability timer expires. Some higher-grade routes offer an optional timed gatekeeper challenge that upgrades the final cache choices when completed.

Clear flow

  1. Find an active grade-labeled gate and carry its matching Planar Mark.
  2. Enter after the destination stabilizes; your mark is consumed only when admission succeeds.
  3. Clear the rooms and defeat the gatekeeper before the stability limit.
  4. Choose one Armament, Planar, or Cartographer Cache when the reward window appears. If you close it, use /breachreward before collapse.
  5. Leave through the exit rift. The cleared pocket collapses five minutes later and ejects anyone still inside.

Rewards

Breach enemies and gatekeepers can drop graded gear. Completing an expedition presents three cache types: Armament for equipment, Planar for planar materials, or Cartographer for maps and exploration rewards. Choose one cache per run. The optional challenge upgrades each choice when its conditions are met.
