What's New

Recent changes installed on the live Nosivad shard. This page tracks player-facing features; the in-game windows show current spell and recipe values.

November 16, 2026 — Magic update

  • Spellbooks now offer school browsing together with Known and Missing filters. Magery remains available alongside Necromancy, Evocation, Warding, Conjuration, and Verdancy.
  • New specialist-school spells, inscription scrolls, travel spells, mounts, and a Verdancy trainer expand the magic paths. See Magic and Spellbooks for the school roles.
  • Necromancy corpse-based skeleton, zombie, and spirit summons now have templates for every E through S corpse grade.
  • Rune Mark now handles an old unresolved destination without blocking a valid remark, while still protecting valid static, buried, and outlaw-only runes.
  • Blood Ingot keeps its 100 Metalsmithing requirement and now has a valid crafting chance range at that skill level.

November 12, 2026 — Inventory and crafting

  • /pack opens a read-only backpack organizer; /inventory and /organizer are aliases.
  • Crafting order bulk fill recognizes material variants of Gathering Blades and legacy Gatherer's Scythes.
  • Steel, Shadow, Rift, and Blood smelting recipe scrolls are available as crafting order rewards.
  • The Sewer Dungeon enforces the indoor mount rule.

See Skills and Stats, Reference, and Breach Gates for current mechanics.
