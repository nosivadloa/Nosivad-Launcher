Professions

Profession trainer chains lead from Apprentice through Grandmaster.

Ranks and requirements

Each chain has Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, and Grandmaster tiers. Its listed skills must reach 30, 50, 80, and 100 respectively, and each earlier quest must be completed. Speak to the trainer or guild NPC named by your active quest. Existing profession progress is retained.

Choose a discipline

  • Combat: Fighter, Rogue, Mage, Evocator, Warder, Conjurer, Necromancer, Tamer, and Bard.
  • Crafting: Blacksmith, Carpenter, Tailor, Alchemist, Scribe, and Chef.
  • Gathering: Miner, Fisher, and Lumberjack.

The in-game /professions or /economy window shows your current identities, ranks, and next tier. Combat ability books can require the matching rank. Blacksmith and Tailor ranks improve relevant planar salvage and reduce repair Dust costs.
