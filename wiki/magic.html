Magic and Spellbooks

Nosivad keeps familiar Magery while adding five specialist schools. The spellbook shows your learned spells and lets you browse by school or filter known and missing spells.

Schools

SchoolFocus
MageryClassic utility and travel, including Mark, Recall, and Portal.
NecromancyCorpse magic, spirits, undead servants, and grave-themed spells.
EvocationElemental attacks, area spells, and the Ember Steed.
WardingProtection, cleansing, healing, and group support.
ConjurationSummons, rift travel, and recalling your active conjured allies.
VerdancyPlant and animal magic, nature effects, and a Verdant Bear mount.

Learning spells

Use the appropriate inscription scroll to learn a spell, then find it in the spellbook. The school filter and Known/Missing filter can be used together. Some familiar Magery spells also have distinct specialist-school versions; check the skill shown on each spell rather than assuming that similarly named spells use the same skill.

Summons and corpse grades

Conjuration and Verdancy summons count toward pet capacity. Conjuration can recall its active summons. Necromancy's corpse-based skeleton, zombie, and spirit servants use the source corpse's E through S grade when choosing a summon variant; stronger grades can require more pet slots. The game checks capacity before a new servant appears.

Travel and rune marking

Magery handles standard Mark, Recall, and Portal travel. Keep a rune in your backpack to mark it at an eligible location. A valid static destination, a buried rune, or an outlaw-only rune remains protected from remarking.
