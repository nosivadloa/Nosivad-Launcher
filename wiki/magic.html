Magic and Spellbooks

Nosivad has familiar Magery and five specialist schools. Each specialist school uses its own casting skill, spell list, inscription scrolls, and progression.

Spellbooks and learning

Browse a school in the spellbook, then use the Known or Missing filter to see what you can already cast or still need to learn. Use the corresponding inscription scroll to learn a spell. Similar names can belong to different schools; read the skill shown on the spell. The spellbook and scroll show current circle and mana cost.

Magery

Magery keeps the classic spell path, including familiar utility and travel such as Mark, Recall, and Portal. Specialist versions of some effects have separate spell entries and use the specialist skill.

Evocation — elemental offense

Evocation emphasizes damage and elemental pressure. Early focused casts include Cinderbolt, Glacial Spear, and Storm Lance. Pillar of Fire, Flamewave, and Ice Rain cover areas; Thunderclap provides a heavier single-target lightning strike. Elemental Convergence deals extra damage when the target already has burning, chill, or shock effects, rewarding a sequence of casts. Evocation also has Cinderlight and the temporary Summon Ember Steed mount.

Warding — defense and recovery

Warding protects and restores allies. Aegis and Bulwark add defense, Repel reflects a limited amount of spell damage, and Purifying Ward cleanses poison while adding brief protection. Heal, Greater Heal, and Resurrect use Warding on this path. Circle of Mending and Circle of Purity support nearby allies. Warding also has a focused force attack, Sanctuary's Grace as a high-circle healing effect, Warden's Light, and the temporary Call Guardian Charger mount.

Conjuration — bound allies and control

Start with Arcane Dart for direct force damage and Aetheric Snare to briefly slow one enemy. Conjure Cinder Sentinel, Call Frostbound Hound, Call Arcane Wisp, and Conjure Ashen Acolyte offer different guardian, striker, and caster roles. Energy Vortex and Arcane Storm are location summons. The Circle VIII Rift Colossus is a powerful guardian that uses all five magical summon slots.

Rally the Bound increases your active magical summons' attack speed; Bound Aegis raises their defense; Mend the Bound heals injured Conjuration summons. Recall the Bound brings active Conjuration summons back beside you. Black Hole pulls hostile creatures inward and damages them near its center. Conjuration also has Conjured Beacon and Call Astral Steed.

Verdancy — nature, forms, and beasts

Verdancy mixes nature attacks and support: Briar Dart, Bramble Snare, Verdant Mend, Barkskin, Purify, Circle of Spring, Virulent Bloom, and Briarstorm. Temporary beast aspects progress from Wolf and Bear through Grove Stalker, Dire Wolf, Great Bear, and Primal Avatar. Forms share one active aspect at a time. Summon Spirit Wolf, Call Sproutling, and Call Ancient Treant provide nature allies. Verdant Light and Call Verdant Bear add utility and travel.

Necromancy — corpses and grave magic

Necromancy uses grave-themed attacks, wards, and corpse rites. Animate Flesh, Animate Bones, and Extract Spirit create temporary servants from valid corpses. The corpse's E–S grade determines the servant variant. Soul Siphon, Frailty, Corpse Explosion, Gravefire, Soul Transfusion, Life Tap, Bone Ward, and Reaper's Covenant broaden the path. Gravebound Renewal heals injured Necromancy summons.

Summon capacity

Magical summons share a five-summon and five-slot limit, with stronger allies consuming more slots. Conjuration and Verdancy summons also count against pet capacity. The game checks capacity before a new ally appears; release or dismiss an existing summon when at the limit.

School travel and mounts

Each specialist school has its own Mark, Recall, and short teleport pair: Necromancy has Grave Mark, Death's Recall, and Grave Step; Evocation has Ember Mark, Ashen Recall, and Flashstep; Warding has Warden's Mark, Sanctuary Recall, and Aegis Step; Conjuration has Rift Anchor, Wayfarer's Recall, and Riftstep; Verdancy has Verdant Mark, Roots Return, and Wildstep. Each school also has a temporary mount spell. Keep a rune in your backpack to mark an eligible location; protected static, buried, and outlaw-only runes cannot be freely remarked.
