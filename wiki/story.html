Story and Investigation

Follow the persistent Chapter I investigation through the world.

The Unseen Thread

  1. Find three environmental clues during exploration.
  2. Ask Pip Merrymarch to decode the hidden cadence.
  3. Report the result to a regional leader.

Use /story to review discovered clues and rumors. The journal remembers your progress across sessions. Later clues can depend on shared world consequences from major events, so another player's actions can alter the aftermath visible to you.

Pip performs at the Eldeir hearth and also offers rotating musical buffs. The investigation can be followed alongside contracts and regional events.
