Skills and Stats

Nosivad keeps familiar Legends of Aria skills while expanding the room available for long-term character growth.

Character limits

  • Individual skills cap at 100.
  • Counted skills share an 800-point total cap.
  • Individual stats cap at 150, with a 450-point total cap.

Barding and Provoke

Provoke uses the visible Barding skill together with Musicianship. Its difficulty reflects both selected creatures: harmless wildlife are easiest, starter enemies are approachable for developing bards, intermediate monsters demand practiced skill, dangerous monsters challenge advanced bards, and bosses remain difficulty 100.

Successful Provoke practice receives a scoped 2x Barding gain multiplier. This improves Barding practice only; it does not double the chance that Provoke succeeds and does not alter other skill gains.

Crafting at the skill cap

Blood Ingots require 100 Metalsmithing skill. Reaching the requirement makes the recipe available; the crafting window shows its success chance. Steel, Shadow, Rift, and Blood smelting recipe scrolls can be earned through crafting orders.

Vitality

Vitality supports skill growth and grants stronger stat bonuses when well rested. Recover vitality near an inn hearth while a bard performs. Low vitality blocks skill and stat gains until you rest.
