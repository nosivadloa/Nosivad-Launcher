Markets and Crafting

Trade player goods and turn rift materials into lasting equipment.

Player markets

Player market boards are active in Upper Plains, Eastern Frontier, and Southern Rim. Other regions do not currently have an enabled board. Check a board in person for listings and current prices.

Crafting orders

Crafting order NPCs provide commissions and a daily refresh route. Orders contribute to Vocation Renown. Gathering Blade material variants and legacy Gatherer's Scythes count for supported bulk-fill orders. Steel, Shadow, Rift, and Blood smelting recipe scrolls can appear as order rewards.

Planar salvage and forge

The Arcane Recycler at the Eldeir smithy converts Riftborn Gear into Planar Dust, Shards, and qualifying high-grade Cores. Weapon and armor salvage requires an Arcane Anvil nearby; other Fabrication salvage uses a reusable Arcane Toolkit. The Planar Forge can repair, reroll, cleanse, improve an eligible grade, or awaken an A/S relic trait. Its window displays material costs and asks for confirmation before permanent changes.

See Loot and Relics for gear grades and Masterworks.
