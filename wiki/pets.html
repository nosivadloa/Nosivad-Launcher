Pets and Companions

Build a trained pet or hire a traveling adventurer.

Pet progression

Pets have persistent levels from 1 to 100. At levels 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100, choose a learned ability from the options for that pet's family. These learned choices are separate from the pet template's native first ability. Open /pet or use a Tamer's Grimoire to inspect the current pet and unspent choices.

Hireable adventurers

Autonomous adventurers follow regional roads, fight, loot, restock, and sell. Hire an available adventurer for 30 minutes, then set Follow or Guard and Defensive, Balanced, or Aggressive tactics in conversation. Renewing the same hire builds persistent loyalty and a renewal discount; higher loyalty also extends the wait after an employer disconnects.

Adventurer classes include Vanguard, Ranger, Arcanist, Shadowblade, and Templar. Their three class skills grow through relevant successful actions and persist across replacement respawns.
