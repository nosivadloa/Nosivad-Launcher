Vocation Renown

A shared level 1–100 progression path for gathering and crafting.

Progress

Successful gathering, eligible crafts, repairs, and crafting orders contribute to Vocation Renown. Your gathering and crafting contributions are tracked separately within the shared progression. Use /vocation or /tradelevel to view level, rank, and available Trade Insights.

Trade Insights

One Insight is earned every five Vocation levels. Insight choices include gathering assignments, regional surveys, commission options, order refresh benefits, and production records. The in-game Trade Insights window gives each node's current cost and requirement.

Orders and surveys

Crafting order NPCs offer commissions and a daily refresh route. A basic Gathering Journal survey asks for 60 successful actions in its assigned region and awards 500 Vocation XP; advanced surveys require 100 and award 1,000 XP. Check the journal before gathering so your actions count in the intended region.

Vocation levels and profession certification are separate: trainer quests still control profession ranks. See Professions.
