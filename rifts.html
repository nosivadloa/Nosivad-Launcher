Legends of Aria: EternalNosivad shard details

This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Planar Rifts

Standard rifts are static world anchors. Players travel to them, read an Ancient Map Scroll, and face enemies that spill into the world in waves.

How Rifts Work

  1. A player finds or receives an Ancient Map Scroll and keeps it in their backpack.
  2. The player stands within 8 units of a static Rift Anchor.
  3. The player uses the scroll and presses Read to open a portal above the rift crystal.
  4. Enemies emerge one or two at a time for a short period.
  5. The portal closes after the encounter finishes.

Anchor Themes

Rifts are placed in locations that match their creature theme. Undead rifts belong in crypts and ruins, infernal rifts near scorched or corrupted spaces, beast rifts in wilderness, and sewer rifts in the starter sewer route.

Rift Encounters

Rift Anchor type alone determines which creature profile answers the scroll. Ancient Map Scroll Grades set encounter difficulty and rewards; scrolls no longer carry random creature variants.

Rewards

Rift enemies are stronger than normal creatures in the same area and can carry improved loot, Adventurer XP, map scroll drops, and rare relic chances. Completing the entire encounter awards participating players a Planar Mark matching its Grade. Marks can be exchanged for planar materials and items; higher Grade offers may require more marks.
