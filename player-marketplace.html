Regional Player Marketplace

Players set the prices. Each active market keeps its own order book, escrow, proceeds, and claims.

The first active marketplaces are in Eldeir, Helm, and Pyros. Orders do not cross regional boundaries.

The blue-roof Township Boards in these towns now open the Player Marketplace. Their regional artwork, locations, and rotations remain familiar. The steward beside each board explains the local ledger, opens the nearby market, and shares regional gossip.

Create Sell Order

  1. Use the regional marketplace board and choose Create Sell Order.
  2. Select the exact item or complete stack to escrow.
  3. Set the unit price and confirm the listing.
  4. Return to My Orders to review or cancel it.

Exact equipment and non-standard items remain the same objects while escrowed. Cancelling or expiring an order sends the remaining item to Claims.

Create Buy Order

  1. Choose Create Buy Order.
  2. Select a stackable commodity as the item specification.
  3. Choose the quantity and maximum unit price.
  4. The required gold is reserved when the order is created.

Sellers can fill a compatible buy order with a matching stack. Price-time priority determines which eligible order is filled first.

Fees, Tax, and Expiration

  • Listing fee: 2%, with a minimum of one gold.
  • Sales tax: 4% of completed sale proceeds.
  • Default order duration: 7 days.
  • Maximum active orders: 30 per player in each regional ledger.

Claims and Safety

Completed proceeds, purchased items, refunds, cancellations, and expired escrow are delivered through the board's Claims tab. Claims remain regional, so return to the same market where the order was created.

  • Players cannot trade with their own orders.
  • Buy orders are limited to standardized stackable goods.
  • Quest, bound, unique, graded, relic, Masterwork, Rift, Breach, and other protected items are rejected.
  • Ambiguous recovery is frozen for staff review instead of risking duplicate or lost value.

Player and Staff Controls

Players see Sell Orders, Buy Orders, My Orders, Claims, History, Create Sell Order, and Create Buy Order. Audit, Test, Freeze, refund, and recovery controls are visible only to authorized staff.
