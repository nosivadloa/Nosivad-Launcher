Weapon Armory

The current server-defined weapon types, base attack and speed, and the primary and secondary abilities shown in the in-game weapon book.

The equipped item's WeaponType determines these abilities. Material, grade, rarity, generated name, and enchantments do not replace the type's primary or secondary ability. Relics keep the abilities of their underlying type unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Leather Quivers and Arrow Recovery

Fabricators can craft a Leather Quiver from four Leather at 20 Fabrication. It accepts the four native arrow types, holds up to eight distinct stacks, and stores those arrows at half their normal weight. Removing arrows restores their ordinary loose weight.

  • Arrows remain finite: the quiver never creates ammunition.
  • An arrow from a successful normal bow attack or ranged ability is recorded on a non-player creature and appears as an ordinary loose-arrow stack in its corpse when it dies.
  • A missed shot is lost.
  • Recovered arrows can be dropped directly onto a closed quiver and merge with a compatible stored stack.
  • Quivers cannot be sold to NPCs or salvaged.

Nosivad-Generated and Unique Weapons

Random Riftborn names, grade, rarity, and enchantments are rolled when an item is created, but its abilities still come from its base type. The current generated weapon pool uses Longsword, Broadsword, Katana, Mace, War Mace, Quarterstaff, Magic Staff, Magic Wand, Warfork, Short Bow, and Long Bow.

Examples: Blade of the First Rift and the Riftsteel Armament use Longsword abilities; Bow of Planar Paths uses Long Bow abilities; Sewer Bone Shiv uses Bone Dagger abilities; Drainpipe Mace uses Mace abilities. Staff of the Veil and Focus of Torm are Magic Staff and Magic Wand relics and therefore have no default primary or secondary weapon ability.

This page is derived from the current installed server equipment and weapon-ability tables, not the discontinued community wiki. The in-game weapon book remains authoritative for the exact item currently equipped.
