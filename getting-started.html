Getting Started

New characters begin their Nosivad journey around Eldeir Village, a lower-pressure area built to teach the shard's custom systems without overwhelming new players.

Lost? Use /activities for the combined activity hub, /adventure for your exact objective and Track button, /level for character progress, and /help for the full in-game guide.

The First-Hour Loop

  1. Find Mira Vale near Eldeir's marketplace at 237.97, 838.62 and open the Eldeir Pathfinder.
  2. Complete Rat Catching on the north field path.
  3. Enter the sewer through the inn hatch near 247.6, 869.3, push into the deeper chambers, and gather Spider Silk from the larger Silk Mother Spiders. Ordinary Lurkers do not carry usable silk.
  4. Return above ground for Wolf Trouble on the outer trail.
  5. Re-enter the sewer for Sewer Scout, Watching Eyes, and Wing Samples.
  6. Challenge the Sewer Broodlord as your first dungeon capstone.
  7. Complete First Low Rift at the sewer anchor to earn your first E-Grade Planar Mark.
  8. Claim original Eldeir path rewards through Mira, then report completed regional service to Mayor Orville for renown.

Early Advice

Do not rush into deep rifts. Learn healing, retreat paths, God Shrine use, contract turn-ins, and how rift waves spawn before taking on harder encounters. Rest near the Eldeir inn bard's hearth to recover vitality. A character can hold one Nosivad contract at a time, and regional work must be completed in the issuing region. Early gear comes more reliably from path rewards, Mayor Orville's renown ranks, and the Sewer Broodlord than from ordinary weak creatures.

Interface Choices

Open /chat to toggle Global messages and optional regional-event notices; /global off, /global on, and /events alerts on or /events alerts off remain available as direct commands. Say remains nearby speech; select Global or use /global <message> for shard-wide chat. Routine server information is routed to the System tab so the Chat tab remains easier to follow.
