Getting Started

New characters begin their Nosivad journey around Eldeir Village, a lower-pressure area built to teach the shard's custom systems without overwhelming new players.

Lost? Use /activities for the combined activity hub, /adventure for your exact objective and Track button, /level for character progress, and /help for the full in-game guide.

The First-Hour Loop

  1. Find Mira Vale beside the bounty board near 258, 887 and open the Eldeir Pathfinder.
  2. Complete Rat Catching on the north field path.
  3. Enter the sewer through the inn hatch near 247.6, 869.3 and gather Spider Silk from the larger Silk Mother Spiders.
  4. Return above ground for Wolf Trouble on the outer trail.
  5. Re-enter the sewer for Sewer Scout, Watching Eyes, and Wing Samples.
  6. Challenge the Sewer Broodlord as your first dungeon capstone.
  7. Complete First Low Rift at the sewer anchor to earn your first E-Grade Breach Sigil.
  8. Turn every one-time Eldeir introduction in directly to Mira; use /adventure whenever you lose the thread.

Early Advice

Use /hud full, /hud compact, or /hud off to save the shortcut-bar layout you prefer. Use /events alerts on or /events alerts off to control one optional notice when a new regional event begins.

Do not rush into deep rifts. Learn healing, retreat paths, contract turn-ins, sewer pacing, and how rift waves spawn before taking on harder encounters. A character can hold one Nosivad contract at a time, and regional work must be completed in the board's region. Early gear comes more reliably from Mira's rewards and the Sewer Broodlord than from ordinary weak creatures.
