This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Chapter I: The Unseen Thread

Nosivad's first persistent investigation connects rifts, relics, invisible corruption, and an intelligence watching from beyond the planar boundary.

How to Investigate

  1. Use /story, /lore, or /journal to open your persistent Story Journal. Select Track Next Clue for a personal waypoint to the next available clue.
  2. Explore roads, ruins, camps, rift scars, and other quiet places for unusual evidence.
  3. Record any three clues. This progress belongs to your character and survives travel and restarts.
  4. Find Pip Merrymarch and choose Ask About Strange Clues.
  5. Take Pip's decoded refrain to any regional leader and choose Discuss Evidence.
This is exploration content. Discoveries require observation and travel, not a combat grade or boss kill. Tracking uses one personal waypoint at a time; a clue in another region first tells you where to travel.

What the Evidence Suggests

Rifts are wounds in the boundary, but relics may not be their cause. Early evidence suggests relics resonate with a presence already pressing against the world. Corruption can exist before it becomes visible, first betrayed by altered shadows, delayed reflections, strange animal behavior, repeated sounds, and records that contradict the landscape.

Shared World Consequences

Completed regional events and defeated world bosses now leave persistent entries in the shard's story state. Unique outcomes raise a shared consequence level. As that level grows, new aftermath evidence physically appears in affected regions and the next story chapter can unlock.

  • Every player contributes to the same evolving world state.
  • Your discoveries and completed chapter remain personal and persistent.
  • Later clues may exist because another adventurer changed the world.
  • The Story Journal shows which aftermath clues are visible without revealing exact coordinates.

People Remember

Pip, regional leaders, and roaming Adventurers now respond to your investigation stage. Their rumors change after you find evidence, decode the hidden cadence, report the discovery, or witness new consequences. Completing Chapter I awards a Lorekeeper's Lens and opens the aftermath phase.

Field Advice

  • Read object names and tooltips; meaningful evidence uses a violet title.
  • Check places where an event recently ended, especially where light, ice, ash, or shadows could preserve a trace.
  • Talk to Adventurers even when you do not intend to hire them.
  • Reopen /story after major server events to see what the shard has exposed.
