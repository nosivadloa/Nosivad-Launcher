All Skills (47)

Every skill in the installed base Knowledge Base, with Nosivad skill additions and overrides. Individual skills cap at 100; the shared skill pool caps at 800 on Nosivad.

Skill descriptions explain what training changes. Your Skills window shows current value, cap, and gain direction. Combat certification and magic schools have additional quest or spell requirements.

Combat and magic (31)

Animal Lore

With Animal Taming, decides which pets you can control; controllable pets always obey. Soothe improves taming. Animal Lore takes Vigor's place when you bandage pets, and strengthens pet abilities. At 80, with 80 Healing, bandages can bring fallen pets back to life. Study Creature reveals more of a creature as Animal Lore rises.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Animal Taming

Tame wild creatures with a crook. Wounding, food, Soothe and the taming game improve your visible chance. Controllable pets always obey. Collect species in your Bestiary.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Archery

Improves your accuracy with bows, and your evasion while holding one. Bow damage hits cloth and unarmored targets harder; heavy armor and shields resist it.

Primary stat: Strength

Barding

Calms a creature, or turns it on another, with music; your skill is tested against the creature's difficulty. Needs an instrument. Players, pets and bosses can't be swayed.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Bashing

Improves your accuracy with maces, hammers, mauls and quarterstaves, and your evasion while wielding them. Bashing damage hits heavy armor harder, and light armor resists it.

Primary stat: Strength

Beastmastery

Makes your pets hit harder when their target is within 30 of you: half damage at 0 skill, normal at 50 and 150% at 100. Also strengthens pet abilities.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Blocking

With a shield equipped, gives a chance to block physical attacks and arrows, about 30% at 100. A block adds the shield's armor to your defense against that hit. Magic can't be blocked.

Primary stat: Agility

Brawling

Raises your unarmed attack, from 10 at 0 skill to 16 at 100, and your accuracy and evasion when fighting bare-handed. Unarmed blows deal bashing damage.

Primary stat: Strength

Channeling

Increases your mana regeneration, with an extra 10% at 100. Light armor halves the bonus and heavy armor nearly cancels it. Raised by casting beneficial spells from a spellbook.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Conjuration

Binds summoned entities to the battlefield. Higher Conjuration improves the success of calling temporary allies.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Entertainment

The performing half of songs and riddles. With Musicianship it decides whether each one succeeds, and it sets their strength: full at 100 skill, half at 50. Needs a songbook.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Evocation

Shapes raw elemental force into destructive spells. Higher Evocation improves cast success and supports stronger elemental magic.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Healing

Increases how much your bandages heal, together with Vigor (Animal Lore on pets); other players receive half. At 60, bandages also cure poison. At 80, with 80 Vigor or Animal Lore, you can bandage fallen players and pets back to life.

Primary stat: Agility

Hiding

Hides you from sight when nothing hostile within 8 units can see you; your chance equals your skill. Hiding dismounts you and slows you. Players right beside you and trackers can still spot you, and staying hidden over 5 minutes leaves traces.

Primary stat: Agility

Lancing

Improves your accuracy with polearms (spears, warforks, voulges, halberds and scythes), and your evasion while wielding them. Polearms deal piercing damage, which hits light armor harder.

Primary stat: Strength

Magery

A general-purpose magical discipline with elemental attacks, protection, support, healing, and travel. Higher Magery improves cast success across this broad range of spells.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Magic Affinity

Raises your spell Power by up to 25 at 100, increasing the damage your spells deal. It doesn't affect healing or Sorcery, and doesn't stack with Inscription's bonus: the higher one counts.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Magical Attunement

Adds spell Power while you wield an attuned staff or wand: your skill divided by its attunement rating, up to +4 with wands and +8 with the best staves at 100. Raised by casting from a spellbook with one equipped.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Marksmanship

Retired. This skill has no effect.

Primary stat: Strength

Martial Prowess

Sets the success chance of Martial abilities, and adds up to +100% of your weapon's base attack against monsters (a quarter against players and their pets, none for bows). It trains only by fighting monsters.

Primary stat: Agility

Musicianship

Your skill with an instrument. It decides whether you play well enough to calm or provoke creatures and, with Entertainment, whether songs and riddles succeed. Playing near a hearth inspires listeners' stats and skill gains: 2%, rising to 5% at 50 and 10% at 75.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Necromancy

Commands the dead and channels grave-born magic. Higher skill improves the control and potency of Necromancy spells.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Piercing

Improves your accuracy with daggers, kryss, poniards and hunting knives, and your evasion while wielding them. Piercing damage hits light armor harder.

Primary stat: Strength

Slashing

Improves your accuracy with swords and axes, and your evasion while wielding them. Slashing damage hits cloth and unarmored foes harder, and heavy armor resists it.

Primary stat: Strength

Sorcery

Powers wisp abilities while you wield a Monolith sorcery weapon or orb. Their damage grows with skill to full at 100, and new abilities unlock at 20, 30, 50, 80 and 90. Affects monsters only.

Primary stat: Wisdom

Spirit Speak

Senses the hidden currents behind magic. Successful spells from every school can train Spirit Speak; at 100 skill, it reduces their mana costs by up to 25%.

Primary stat: Wisdom

Stealth

Lets you move while hidden. Moving drains a 10-point stealth bar and standing still refills it; an empty bar reveals you. Each step risks a reveal, which grows less likely with skill.

Primary stat: Agility

Summoning

Raises how many wisps you hold in combat (1, then 2 at 60, 3 at 90 and 4 at 100, up to your weapon's limit) and how long Sorcery summons last, up to double at 100.

Primary stat: Wisdom

Verdancy

Channels living nature through restorative magic, binding roots, and beast forms. Higher Verdancy strengthens spells and the capabilities granted by animal aspects.

Primary stat: Wisdom

Vigor

Adds to the base attack of every weapon, bows and fists included, up to +100% at 100. Also raises how much your bandages heal people. At 80 Vigor and 80 Healing, bandages can bring fallen players back to life.

Primary stat: Agility

Warding

Raises protective wards and counters hostile magic. Higher Warding improves cast success and supports advanced wards.

Primary stat: Wisdom

Crafting and gathering (11)

Alchemy

Brews cure, healing, mana and stamina potions in three strengths. At 75 you can brew Mending potions and Unstable Concoctions, and at 90 Summon Mount potions. Soul stones that store skills unlock at 50, 70 and 90.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Blacksmithing

Smelts ore into ingots and forges metal weapons, armor, tools, trap kits and, at 75, anvils, forges and stoves. Higher skill rolls better attack and armor bonuses. Rarer metals need more skill but add durability and enchant capacity.

Primary stat: Strength

Carpentry

Turns wood into boards and crafts bows, arrows, shields, furniture, containers, lighting, fences, instruments, garden beds and siege tools; at 75, crafting tables. Ash and Blightwood need more skill but add durability and enchant capacity.

Primary stat: Agility

Cooking

Cooks meat, fish and produce in a pot near a fire, into snacks, meals and banquets. Eating one raises your maximum health, stamina, mana or carrying capacity for 15 minutes. Meals need 30 to 55 skill, banquets 85, and delicacies such as pies and steaks 55 to 100.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Enchanting

Applies essences to crafted weapons, armor and shields at an enchanting table. The item's material sets how much it can hold, and each enchantment makes the next harder. A failure may lose the essence. At 90, 95 and 100, enchantments roll stronger.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Fabrication

Weaves cloth, tans leather, and sews clothing, cloth and leather armor, mage robes, saddles, saddlebags and pouches. Quilted, silk, beast and vile materials need more skill but add durability and enchant capacity.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Fishing

Catches fish with a rod, from Barrel Fish (difficulty 5) up to Razor-Fish (85); schools are easier to fish. Casts can also bring up sunken-treasure maps, with better maps at 20, 40, 60, 70 and 90.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Gem Forging

Finds gems while mining, refines and upgrades them, and crafts and repairs jewelry. Higher skill improves the chance to find a gem; richer ore deposits yield better gem grades. Jewelry can use Copper, Silver, Gold, or Platinum; the metal raises its gem bonus by 0%, 1%, 2.5%, or 5%.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Inscription

Crafts spell scrolls, spellbooks, runebooks, and attuned staves and wands; the strongest need 75 to 90 skill. Also adds spell Power equal to a tenth of your skill when that beats your Magic Affinity bonus.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Lumberjack

Chops wood with a hatchet. Higher skill improves your success and your chance of a double yield. Ash can be cut at 48 skill and Blightwood at 78.

Primary stat: Strength

Mining

Mines stone and ore with a pick. Higher skill improves your success and your chance of a double yield. Copper needs over 15 skill, gold 30, cobalt 40 and obsidian 60.

Primary stat: Strength

UtilityTypeSkill (5)

Lockpicking

Opens locked chests, coffins and treasure with a lockpick, and disarms pressure-plate traps for 5 minutes. You have a chance from 25 below a lock's difficulty and always succeed 25 above it. Doors can't be picked.

Primary stat: Agility

Snooping

While hidden, opens a nearby player's or pet's backpack so you can see and steal from it; your chance equals your skill. Targets are always warned at 50 skill or below, and less often above. Snooping innocents costs reputation.

Primary stat: Agility

Stealing

While snooping a backpack, drag an item out to steal it. Your chance is half your skill, and you can lift up to a tenth of your skill in stones. Failure reveals you. Stealing from innocent players costs reputation and flags you.

Primary stat: Agility

Tracking

Spots hidden players within 5 to 20 units, showing them to you for a few seconds, and marks hidden traps. Follows the footprints of fugitives On the Run and of players hidden over 5 minutes. Hiding and Stealth resist it.

Primary stat: Intelligence

Treasure Hunting

Deciphers treasure maps and guides your shovel to the chest, which unlocks once its guardians fall. You can attempt a map from 25 below its difficulty (Tattered 10, Frayed 30, Fancy 50, Ornate 70, Devious 90), and more skill pinpoints the spot.

Primary stat: Intelligence
