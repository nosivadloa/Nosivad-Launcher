Skills, Stats and Mana

These values describe Nosivad's verified live scripts. Some formulas shown in the base client's WIP Knowledge Base are outdated or were never implemented.

Skill Caps

LimitCurrent ValueWhat It Means
Individual skill100Each skill uses the normal 0–100 scale.
Total skills600You may distribute 600 points across all counted skills.

If an existing character is above 600 after the cap correction, no skill points are deleted automatically. The character cannot gain more skills until the total is reduced to 600 or below with the normal skill sliders. Any individual skill above 100 should also be lowered to 100.

Channeling and Focus

Focus success uses Channeling as its percentage on the 100-point skill scale: 80 Channeling gives an 80% chance, 90 gives 90%, and 100 gives 100%. Focus requires you to stand still and ends when you move or act.

Passive mana regeneration starts with 0.2, then adds a Channeling and Intelligence portion adjusted by the least mana-friendly equipped armor. Reaching 100 Channeling gives that portion a further 10% mastery bonus. Active Focus doubles the armor-adjusted portion. Direct mana-regeneration effects, such as blessings or equipment modifiers, are added separately.

Maximum Mana

Maximum mana is based on Intelligence × 2.5, before direct maximum-mana bonuses and multipliers. Wisdom does not add maximum mana.

Wisdom and Magic Resistance

Wisdom does not add mana regeneration. Instead, every incoming magical hit rolls a Wisdom-based resistance chance. When it succeeds, the Honeycomb Shield effect appears and that hit deals half damage.

The configured base-stat caps are 150 per stat and 450 total. Adventurer-level bonuses and other effects are applied as modifiers, so the final displayed value may include bonuses beyond a character's trained base stat.

Verified correction: Wisdom is a probabilistic magical defense stat—not a mana-pool or mana-regeneration stat.
