Legends of Aria: EternalNosivad shard details

This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Skills, Stats and Mana

These are the character rules used on Nosivad. The base game provides familiar skills and attributes; this shard changes some caps and formulas.

Skill Caps

LimitCurrent ValueWhat It Means
Individual skill100Each skill uses the normal 0–100 scale.
Total skills800You may distribute 800 points across all counted skills.

If an existing character is above 800, no skill points are deleted automatically. The character cannot gain more skills until the total is reduced below 800 with the normal skill sliders. Any individual skill above 100 should also be lowered to 100.

Channeling and Focus

Focus success uses Channeling as its percentage on the 100-point skill scale: 80 Channeling gives an 80% chance, 90 gives 90%, and 100 gives 100%. Focus requires you to stand still and ends when you move or act.

Passive mana regeneration starts with 0.2, then adds a Channeling and Intelligence portion adjusted by the least mana-friendly equipped armor. Reaching 100 Channeling gives that portion a further 10% mastery bonus. Active Focus doubles the armor-adjusted portion. Direct mana-regeneration effects, such as blessings or equipment modifiers, are added separately.

Maximum Mana

Maximum mana is based on Intelligence × 2.5, before direct maximum-mana bonuses and multipliers. Wisdom does not add maximum mana.

Wisdom and Magic Resistance

Wisdom does not add mana regeneration. Instead, every incoming magical hit rolls a Wisdom-based resistance chance. When it succeeds, the Honeycomb Shield effect appears and that hit deals half damage.

The configured base-stat caps are 150 per stat and 450 total. Adventurer-level bonuses and other effects are applied as modifiers, so the final displayed value may include bonuses beyond a character's trained base stat.

Profession Progression

All 14 active profession chains are enabled through Grandmaster. Existing completion is preserved; no profession reset is required.

TierRequired Skill LevelOther Requirement
Apprentice (I)30Complete any introduction quest
Journeyman (II)50Complete Tier I
Master (III)80Complete Tier II
Grandmaster (IV)100Complete Tier III

Combat: Fighter, Mage, Tamer, Rogue, and Bard. Crafting: Blacksmith, Carpenter, Tailor, Alchemist, Scribe, and Chef. Gathering: Miner, Fisher, and Lumberjack. See the Mining and gem guide for ore-vein locations, Mining requirements, harvest chances, and gem grades.

Every listed skill for the profession must meet the tier threshold. For example, Tamer uses Animal Taming and Animal Lore; Mage uses Magery, Magic Affinity, and Channeling. Follow the quest instructions to the named trainer or guild NPC.

Combat ability books, training, hotbar availability, and ability use require the matching earned profession rank. Finding a higher-rank book does not bypass profession quests.

Bard Skill Gains

Barding is a visible skill and counts toward your total skill cap. Provoke can improve it. Any previous Provocation progress transfers to Barding the first time you log in.

Provoke uses two targets: first choose the creature to influence, then choose what it should attack. After a successful instrument performance, the second-target cursor always appears. The final Barding check uses the average difficulty of both targets. Provoke and its instrument lockout both use a one-second cooldown.

Wisdom and Will Training

Wisdom and Will are secondary stats that can continue to increase through eligible skill practice even after a skill reaches 100, up to the 150 base-stat cap.

Magical Attunement Weapons

Magical Attunement uses magical staffs and wands; a melee quarterstaff is not an Attunement weapon. Mage Staffs and Novice's Wands can appear as Riftborn Gear, in Breach Armament Caches, and as boss-signature rewards. Mage Profession I awards a training staff, and advanced artifact variants use schematic-based recipes.

Spell Scrolls and Inscription

Finished spell scrolls do drop from many mage and boss loot tables, but each listed spell has a low independent chance. Inscription is the dependable source: Scribes craft spell scrolls at an Inscription Table from the appropriate blank, frayed, fine, or ancient scroll material plus reagents. Buying from a player Scribe is therefore usually faster than waiting for one exact spell to drop.

All three visible skills count toward the 800-point total. A character at or above 800 cannot gain them—or any other counted skill—until the total is lowered below the cap.

Power Hour and Crafting Gains

Power Hour lasts 60 minutes with a 20-hour claim cooldown; /powerhour and /power show cooldown only. Window disappearance alone does not confirm an active buff.

Skill-gain rolls are separate from crafting success. Successful and failed resource-consuming crafts may train within the recipe's material-adjusted range. Check Vitality and the 100 individual / 800 total caps if gains stop.

Ordinary Magery is reagent-free on Nosivad. Channeling regeneration scales with the expanded maximum-mana pool. Vocation XP is a separate progression system.
