Recent Changes and Availability

Changes since the September 22 wiki publication; reviewed October 3

How to Read This Page

This refresh covers changes since September 22. A deployed update may still require a script reload or normal restart. Pending items below are not promises that their behavior has been verified in game.

Vocation is operating in production, with current gathering XP awards observed. The detailed Vocation guide explains its mechanics; individual advanced features still need normal player testing.

Official v1.04 on a PvE Shard

The September 28 upstream update added Peacemaking, improved Provoke targeting and creature spellcasting, expanded district-event bosses, protected camp caches until defenders are defeated, and improved housing permissions and visible cooldowns. Nosivad remains PvE; upstream PvP ability changes do not enable player combat here.

With an instrument equipped, Peacemaking calms eligible nearby creatures for a difficulty-dependent period. Provoke makes a successful creature pair fight for 20 seconds and uses a 10-second cooldown. Players and pets cannot be selected as the second victim.

Camp keys are retained when a cache cannot yet open. Co-owners have broader everyday plot permissions while ownership changes and moving crates remain owner-only. Bandage cooldown progress survives relogging, and potion hotbar slots display shared cooldowns.

Crafting, Repairs, and Reclamation

Qualified ordinary Repair Gear attempts restore full durability with guaranteed success, lower wear-scaled material costs, and no failed-repair destruction. Weapon-swing and attuned-weapon wear were reduced by 90%; equipment-hit wear by 50%.

The Recycler now has General, Arcane, and Reclaim tabs. Reclaim accepts eligible ordinary looted equipment, valuables, and raw materials. Ten material credit becomes one Reclamation Mark; splitting stacks cannot improve the payout.

Scavenger, Artisan, and Curator material parcels cost 10, 35, and 90 Reclamation Marks. Crafted, quest, unique, blessed/cursed, graded, relic, currency, and otherwise protected objects remain excluded from Reclaim.

Breach creatures no longer receive both generic and dedicated graded-loot rolls. Breach bosses still guarantee one graded item, with at most one additional grade-dependent bonus item. Ordinary world loot and completion rewards keep their own rules.

Skills, Power Hour, and Recovery

Ordinary Magery on Nosivad does not require or consume reagents. Existing reagent-cost-reduction gear has no benefit for those casts; this does not remove reagent resources from the world.

Power Hour lasts 60 minutes and has a 20-hour claim cooldown. /powerhour and /power show claim availability, not proof that the buff is active. Closing the claim window alone is not confirmation; check the buff icon and skill-check bonus.

Skill gains remain chance-based and separate from crafting success. Both successful and failed resource-consuming crafts can train an eligible native skill. Low Vitality, resting at a campfire, and skill caps can block gains.

/rest restores one Vitality every 30 uninterrupted seconds. A reusable Camping Kit improves that to every 15 seconds. Fabrication 20 crafts the kit from 5 Cloth, 2 Leather, and 2 Boards. Movement, combat, mounting, death, or hearth/performance recovery stops rest.

Quests, Companions, and Everyday Interactions

Mage I advances at 10 Magery, Magic Affinity, or Channeling instead of the unreliable Complete a Mission objective. The stablemaster's one-time horse remains available after Young Player status ends.

Double-clicking a spellbook opens it; equipping is a separate action. Nemesis confirmation identifies its target and duration. Returning corpse owners receive prompt visibility updates, while cosmetic skeleton hair is not loot.

Hired Adventurers clear stale autonomous behavior, follow and catch up, and assist against an owner's hostile target. Stuck road recovery uses a separate cooldown per NPC, reducing repeated recovery work.

Merchant carts retain multiple selected items until one Accept. Gathering missions again permit the normal skilled bonus-resource chance. Same-bank pouch moves do not incorrectly add bank weight.

/stuck shows its 20-second delay. Healing cooldown use no longer repeatedly opens targeting; selecting a healthy patient ends cleanly. Waste Management clears prior waves before the Giant Lurker, and boss defeat completes its progress.

Carpenter II identifies the Belhaven Architect as the Woodsmith turn-in. Pip is protected from the Eldeir guard conflict. The Oasis Unbound Breach uses inland placement; Pad's Valus alchemy services and Nectar selection were restored.

Deployed Follow-ups Awaiting Confirmation

The Eldeir Market/Magetower shortcut replaces the initial ladders with visible two-way portals at 248.72,0,834.18 and 269.38,0,804.52. Stand nearby and double-click; Recall, runes, and charges are not needed. Runtime travel verification is pending.

All six crafting-station recipes were added to Woodsmith random crafting-order rewards and the targeted 12 Commission Mark recipe shop. Carpenter I now searches all active missions for the qualifying Lumber Harvesting Mission, and harvest settlement saves completion before reevaluating quests. Activation confirmation is pending.

The initial Evoker achievement claim has a targeted correction for a pending reward that displayed a notification but did not complete. Activation confirmation is pending.

The shared regional-event pacing change uses a three-to-five-hour shard cooldown, with at most two active regions. It is separate from Breach and world-boss scheduling; runtime timing confirmation is pending.

Investigations and Future Work

A reported stall in cotton collection has a source-validated fix. Do not assume it is resolved solely from this documentation; completion, plant removal, respawn, and mission settlement require gameplay confirmation.

The reported absent Power Hour icon remains an investigation. The skill-gain checks did not show globally disabled crafting gains, but did not establish the affected player's live cause.

Riftborn evolving dragons, their attunements, branching rituals, persistent Codex, and visual variants are design work. They are not the existing Tamer's Grimoire feature and are not available to obtain from this guide.
