AFK and PvE Conduct

  • AFK play is allowed at your own risk. The world remains active, and ordinary predators or corrupted creatures may attack and kill an unattended character.
  • PvE prevents normal player-versus-player combat; it does not make an idle character immune to creatures.
  • Deliberately luring creatures onto another player or using another character to manipulate combat is griefing and is not allowed. Report suspected abuse with /bug and include the time, place, and names involved.
  • More players standing together do not raise corruption's per-creature roll. Players determine which nearby world area is scanned; every eligible creature keeps the same fixed roll, subject to the regional corrupted-host cap.

Secure House Storage

Locked-down secure chests inside a player house may be arranged vertically with Move Object. When nearby house construction or decoration blocks the normal sight ray, an authorized player standing within normal interaction range can still open the chest, move its contained items, and split contained stacks.

  • The exception applies only to a top-level locked-down secure container belonging to a player house.
  • The owner, co-owner, or a friend permitted by the house's container access setting must have normal object-control permission.
  • Locks, range, trade, bank, merchant, noloot, weight, capacity, and other native safety checks remain enforced.
  • Ordinary containers, remote chests, nested containers, and secure house chests the player does not control still use normal visibility and permission rules.

Common Terms

TermMeaning
Planar RiftA static world encounter where themed enemy waves enter the normal world.
Rift BreachA scheduled gate event that opens passage to the PvE Fallen Realm.
Planar MarkA currency used to exchange for planar materials and items.
Regional EventA rotating local encounter featuring a region's enemy families, resources, rewards, and named boss.
Player MarketplaceA regional player-priced order book. Orders, escrow, proceeds, and claims remain in the market where they were created.
Market StewardA lore and guidance NPC who explains and opens the nearby regional marketplace; the board owns all trading.
Daily ContractA board contract claimable once per server calendar day; it returns at server midnight.
Regional ContractA contract whose objective only progresses in the region served by its source.
RenownLocation-specific reputation earned by reporting qualifying service to the correct leader.
Highest StandingThe fifth renown rank at 180 points, which grants that location's permanent benefit.
God ShrineA deity-dedicated replacement for a resurrection shrine that also grants a one-hour blessing.
Pip's RepertoireThree two-minute instrument arrangements with different, mutually exclusive 30-minute buffs balanced to the same 60-point primary-stat budget.
Adventurer GradePermanent E/D/C/B/A/S character progression that controls graded equipment and relative kill XP.
RelicRare named equipment connected to rifts and planar power.
MutationEnemy rarity/power variation from the dynamic enemy system.
World Boss RiftRare temporary rift that spawns one powerful randomized boss.

Vocation and Power Hour Commands

/vocation and /tradelevel open Vocation Renown: shared productive level, category contributions, Trade Insights, commissions, and XP feedback preferences.

/powerhour and /power show the remaining 20-hour claim cooldown. They do not independently verify the active 60-minute buff. /rest starts stationary Vitality recovery. /stuck displays its 20-second rescue delay.
