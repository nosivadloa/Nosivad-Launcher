Nosivad Development Roadmap

Nosivad is growing through focused releases, live player results, and visible follow-up. This page shows what has arrived, what is being tested now, and the larger directions planned for the shard.

Roadmap promise: priorities may move as testing exposes bugs, balance problems, or better opportunities. “Exploring” describes a direction, not a guaranteed release date.
Production milestone — July 29: the six world-event archetypes, connected planar crafting economy, Barding progression correction, Pip's three-instrument repertoire, and tonight's supporting fixes are now live together. Current roadmap work is observation, tuning, and follow-through.

Current Priority Order

Now
Production observation of the six world-event archetypes, Barding progression, the connected planar economy, Pip's repertoire, companion routes, and Breach results.
Next
Event and economy tuning from player results, profession completion, and regional consequences.
Later
New endgame encounters, connected story chapters, guild goals, and prestige collections.
Always
Stability, accessibility, fair progression, and clear communication.

Recently Delivered — First Pass

Combat, Loot & Progression

Breach density and timers received an initial balance pass. Completion rewards, Breach Essence, corrupted enemy XP, and live-result telemetry now scale more intentionally across E through S grade.

Adventurer NPCs & Travel

Adventurer travel expanded to fourteen hubs. Combat judgment, class-aware equipment choices, Arcanist support, hiring orders, tactics, renewals, loyalty discounts, and owner-return grace periods were expanded.

New-Player & Interface Polish

The first-hour route now extends into a unified activity hub, personal contract/event/story/station waypoints, actionable profession requirements, a practical economy view, progressive adventure guidance, persistent HUD layouts, and optional event alerts.

Six World-Event Archetypes — Live

Regional invasions now share the rotation with Trinit's Stoneway Caravan escort, Helm's Violet Iron resource surge, Valus's Lantern Shrine defense, Black Forest's roaming Gloam-Antler, and Pyros's multi-stage Ash-Rift Convergence. Shared objectives, contribution rewards, adaptive cooldowns, and telemetry support live tuning.

Story, Lore & Discovery

Chapter I: The Unseen Thread adds eight persistent environmental clues, a multi-step Pip and leader investigation, evolving rumors, a Lorekeeper reward, and shared event consequences that reveal aftermath clues and unlock future chapters.

Planar Crafting & Economy

Rift materials and Breach Essence now connect combat rewards to recycling, repair, cleansing, affix work, grade improvement, relic awakening, Astral items, Planar Masterworks, and controlled trade.

Barding & Pip's Repertoire

Provoke now uses creature-appropriate Barding difficulty tiers and a scoped practice bonus. Pip rotates among lute, flute, and drum arrangements with three different, mutually exclusive signature buffs.

Active Development

Combat, Loot & Progression

  • Tune Breach difficulty, enemy density, and wave timers using actual group results.
  • Refine grade balance, corruption, relics, rewards, and progression pacing.
  • Improve risk versus reward without making lower-grade content disposable.

Adventurer NPCs & Travel

  • Verify accurate road networks across expanded regional destinations.
  • Improve combat decisions, looting, class identity, and equipment choices.
  • Expand hiring and long-term companion behavior.

Regional Runtime Test Focus

  • Verify escort pathing and ambushes, resource-node interactions, shrine waves, elite patrols, staged rift advancement, and existing invasions in the live world.
  • Measure turnout, objective completion, event duration, expiry rate, contribution eligibility, and reward pacing.
  • Use those results to tune spawn pressure, objective targets, cooldowns, rewards, and future regional consequences.

Economy Runtime Test Focus

  • Measure Planar Dust, Shard, Core, and Breach Essence income against repair, cleansing, reroll, upgrade, relic, and Masterwork costs.
  • Watch Arcane Recycler yields, skilled-crafter service demand, Masterwork trade releases, and whether any reward source or material sink dominates the loop.
  • Tune costs and yields without disconnecting combat rewards from crafting and trade.

Barding and Pip Runtime Test Focus

  • Confirm wildlife, starter enemies, intermediate monsters, dangerous monsters, and bosses produce the intended Barding progression curve.
  • Measure Provoke gains with its scoped two-times practice bonus while preserving the Musicianship and two-target gates.
  • Verify Pip's lute, flute, and drum rotations remain audible, mutually exclusive, and equally useful without following players across regions.

Story Runtime Test Focus

  • Verify clue placement, readability, one-time discovery credit, and persistence across region travel and restarts.
  • Test Pip's three-clue decode, leader completion, Lorekeeper's Lens delivery, and evolving Adventurer rumors.
  • Confirm unique regional events and world-boss defeats expose aftermath clues and advance shared chapter state.

What Comes Next

Dungeons, Breaches & Endgame

More authored layouts, objectives, hazards, bosses, affix combinations, optional high-grade challenge paths, and long-term S-Grade goals.

Professions, Crafting & Economy

All fourteen profession advancement paths are enabled. The current pass adds a profession ledger, Blacksmith/Tailor planar-service mastery, and persistent material-flow telemetry; vendor prices, gathering yields, durability, and drop rates will be tuned only after representative live results.

Parties, Guilds & Community

Clearer group rewards, cooperative contracts, regional objectives, shared accomplishments, guild goals, social spaces, and meaningful challenges for organized players.

Future Story Chapters

Build on The Unseen Thread with the force behind the rifts, rare observation encounters, named-boss memory, visible regional recovery states, and choices that shape later investigations.

Living World & Events

Build on the six live archetypes with invasion chains, rotating conditions, visible regional recovery, objective scaling, late-arrival support, and outcomes that influence later events.

Collections, Housing & Prestige

Trophies, decorations, boss keepsakes, paintings, mounts, collection tracking, display spaces, and cosmetic accomplishments that reward long-term play without unchecked power.

How Player Feedback Shapes the Roadmap

  • Focused playtests identify which systems need evidence rather than guesses.
  • Completion time, deaths, resets, rewards, progression speed, and economic movement guide tuning.
  • Bugs, usability issues, and balance concerns are tracked separately so each receives the right response.
  • Major testing rounds will be followed by summaries explaining what changed and why.
