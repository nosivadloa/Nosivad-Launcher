Nosivad Roadmap

Nosivad develops its shard around exploration, progression, and a living world. Priorities can change as the game and player feedback evolve.

Areas of interest

Adventure

Continue improving the variety and pacing of encounters across the world.

Progression

Refine long-term character and profession goals as the shard grows.

Living world

Explore ways for regional activity and stories to make the world feel more connected.

Community

Look for useful shared goals and activities for players to enjoy together.

These are broad directions only. Systems that are still being designed or developed are not described here in detail. Guides linked from the Knowledge Base cover the features currently presented to players.
