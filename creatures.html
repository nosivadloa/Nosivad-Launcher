Legends of Aria: EternalNosivad shard details

This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Creatures

Nosivad creatures can be ordinary area enemies, dynamic mutated enemies, rift-born enemies, corrupted hosts, or rare world bosses.

Enemy Rarity

Most enemies remain common. Special rarity enemies are intentionally less frequent, especially from natural respawns.

RarityPlayer Meaning
CommonBaseline creature.
UncommonSlightly stronger and more rewarding.
RareNoticeably stronger with better loot odds.
EpicDangerous mutation with high reward potential.
LegendaryVery rare natural threat; treat with caution.

Ancient Map Scroll Drops

Corrupted creatures have a 5% chance to drop an Ancient Map Scroll. Mutations add 1% for Uncommon, 2% for Rare, 4% for Epic, or 7% for Legendary enemies. Corruption and mutation bonuses combine with each other and with a Planar Rift enemy's existing scroll chance, up to a 35% combined creature-drop cap.

Creature Sources

Starter Creatures

Field rats, sewer rats, and restless wolves around Eldeir are tuned to teach combat and bounty flow without flooding new players with money or power.

Rift-Born Creatures

Rift enemies arrive in waves and are slightly stronger than standard creatures in the same area. They grant more experience and have better chances at useful loot than nearby ordinary creatures.

Planar Rift Creatures

The Rift Anchor type alone selects the creature profile that answers an Ancient Map Scroll. The scroll Grade sets encounter difficulty and rewards. Creatures emerging from the rift are identified as Rift-Touched.

World Bosses

World bosses are randomized one-off threats. They are not meant to respawn predictably and should feel like major events.

Rift Corruption

Corrupted hosts are marked by a purple Corrupted Host name/title, a riftborne tooltip, a subtle void aura, and a body roughly twenty percent larger than normal. They are more violent, stronger, worth more XP, and have better loot potential. Bounty boards may offer Purge Rift Corruption contracts for hunting them.

Tameable Companions

Controlled pets have their own level, XP, stat growth, and creature-family abilities. Use /pet to manage them and the Tamer's Grimoire. See the Taming and Pets guide for levels, ability milestones, storage rules, and control-slot costs.

Creature Grade and XP

A creature’s grade reflects its threat and helps determine the experience it awards.
