Rift Breach Expeditions

Breach Gates lead into timed E through S-grade dungeon expeditions. Bring a matching Breach Sigil, fight through occupied rooms and halls, locate hidden bosses, and escape before the pocket collapses.

Breach Sigils And Admission

Every player needs one Breach Sigil matching the gate's grade for first admission to an active expedition. The sigil is consumed on entry. A player already admitted to that same active Breach may reconnect or re-enter without spending another sigil.

  • Mira's one-time First Low Rift contract awards the first E-Grade Breach Sigil.
  • Completing an overworld rift awards every participant a sigil matching that rift's tier.
  • Completing an E through A Breach awards the next grade's sigil when the expedition reward is claimed.
  • Completing S-grade awards another S-Grade Breach Sigil.
  • Players who completed a sigil-awarding contract before sigils existed receive the missing reward automatically once at login.

Recognizing And Using A Gate

Breach Gates are marked by persistent purple void-fire, planar glow, and smoke. Use a grade-labeled gate while carrying a matching sigil. The gate reserves and populates its dedicated dungeon before opening; wait for the success message before trying to enter.

A sigil is not consumed merely because preparation starts. It is consumed on a player's first admission to a successfully opened run. If preparation cannot finish, the gate releases its reservation and reports the failure instead of silently charging admission.

Clear Flow

  1. Use a grade-labeled Breach Gate.
  2. The gate opens that grade's authored expedition in the dedicated breach pocket.
  3. Clear each encounter wave; every wave repopulates all physical dungeon rooms.
  4. Search the occupied rooms for hidden lieutenants and gatekeepers.
  5. Defeat the final gatekeeper to open an individual reward choice for every eligible player.
  6. Choose one cache before the five-minute collapse. If the window is closed, use /breachreward to reopen it.

Room means a physical dungeon chamber, hall means one of its connecting passages, wave means an encounter stage, and assault means a repeated spawn inside a holdout wave. Every wave places low-tier themed minions in all 11 rooms and all 10 halls. Non-boss waves advance after all required mobs die. Boss waves advance when the hidden target dies, removing surviving creatures before the next wave repopulates the dungeon.

Non-boss waves have grade-scaled timers from 10 minutes at E-grade to 5 minutes at S-grade. At timeout, clearing more than 50% advances the expedition; clearing exactly 50% or less resets and repopulates the same wave after five seconds. Boss and lieutenant waves are untimed.

The entire Breach also has a hard stability limit: 30 minutes at E, 35 at D, 40 at C, 45 at B, 50 at A, and 60 at S. Wave resets never extend it. An unfinished Breach closes without rewards at expiration, ejecting players and destroying the pocket. Warnings appear with 10, 5, and 1 minute remaining.

Use /breach from any region to see which Breach is open, its originating gate region and coordinates, current wave and assault, clear progress, timeout outcome, wave timer, and complete-Breach timer. Use Refresh Status for the newest state and Close when you are finished viewing the window.

Armament Cache

The reliable equipment choice. It grants one random weapon or armor piece as guaranteed Riftborn Gear. The item's required grade matches the completed expedition, its equipment type is random, and its bonuses are randomized within that grade's power budget. Choose Armament when you want an immediate equipment upgrade or another Riftborn item to evaluate.

Planar Cache

The essence and high-roll choice. It always grants additional Breach Essence and also rolls for a random relic. The relic is not guaranteed, so Planar is less predictable than Armament.

Cartographer Cache

The exploration choice. It grants one Ancient Map Scroll matched to the expedition tier and gives that scroll a random rift affix.

Optional Challenge Upgrade

The C-, B-, A-, and S-grade timed challenges upgrade the selected cache: Armament gains one additional Breach Essence, Planar gains additional Essence and improved relic odds, and Cartographer grants a second map scroll. E- and D-grade expeditions currently have no optional reward challenge.
