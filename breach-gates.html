Rift Breach Expeditions

Breach Gates lead into timed E through S-grade dungeon expeditions. Bring a matching Breach Sigil, fight through occupied rooms and halls, locate hidden bosses, and escape before the pocket collapses.

Breach Sigils And Admission

Every player needs one Breach Sigil matching the gate's grade for first admission to an active expedition. The sigil is consumed on entry. A player already admitted to that same active Breach may reconnect or re-enter without spending another sigil.

  • Mira's one-time First Low Rift contract awards the first E-Grade Breach Sigil.
  • Completing an overworld rift awards every participant a sigil matching that rift's tier.
  • Completing an E through A Breach awards the next grade's sigil when the expedition reward is claimed.
  • Completing S-grade awards another S-Grade Breach Sigil.
  • Players who completed a sigil-awarding contract before sigils existed receive the missing reward automatically once at login.

Clear Flow

  1. Use a grade-labeled Breach Gate.
  2. The gate opens that grade's authored expedition in the dedicated breach pocket.
  3. Clear each room in sequence; later rooms and the final boss do not spawn early.
  4. Defeat the gatekeeper to open an individual reward choice for every eligible player.
  5. Choose one cache before the five-minute collapse. If the window is closed, use /breachreward to reopen it.

Boss Loot And Completion Rewards

The gatekeeper's corpse loot is separate from the completion cache. Breach bosses retain their guaranteed graded-equipment drop, while each player also receives the grade's base gold, Breach Essence, and progression sigil after choosing one cache.

Armament Cache

The reliable equipment choice. It grants one random weapon or armor piece as guaranteed graded equipment. The item's required grade matches the completed expedition, its equipment type is random, and its bonuses are randomized within that grade's power budget. Choose Armament when you want an immediate equipment upgrade or another graded item to evaluate.

Planar Cache

The essence and high-roll choice. It always grants additional Breach Essence and also rolls for a random relic. The relic is not guaranteed, so Planar is less predictable than Armament.

Cartographer Cache

The exploration choice. It grants one Ancient Map Scroll matched to the expedition tier and gives that scroll a random rift affix.

Optional Challenge Upgrade

The current C-grade timed challenge upgrades the selected cache: Armament gains one additional Breach Essence, Planar becomes three additional Essence with a 30% relic chance, and Cartographer grants a second map scroll. E- and D-grade expeditions currently have no optional reward challenge.
