Legends of Aria: EternalNosivad shard details

This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Rift Breach and the Fallen Realm

Rift Breach is the scheduled gate event that opens the way to the Fallen Realm. It opens passage to the Fallen Realm, a PvE region protected by Nosivad's player-safety rules.

Rift Breach

The event uses the native gate schedule, opening window, and entry behavior. Use /events for current regional notices and event information.

The Fallen Realm

The gate leads to the Fallen Realm, a PvE area with an infernal population and additional realm-themed creatures. The realm keeps its infernal population and adds Rift Screechers, Veil Hexers, Bone Reavers, and Lantern Wisps with Fallen Realm names. Player-versus-player damage is disabled there.

Planar Marks

Planar Marks are exchange currency earned through planar activities. Use a mark to exchange for planar materials and items. Higher Grade marks unlock rarer offers, and better rewards can require multiple marks.

Ancient Map Scrolls and Rift Anchors

Ancient Map Scrolls have Grades that set encounter difficulty and rewards. The Rift Anchor type determines which creatures answer a scroll. Continue opening rifts to earn marks and planar rewards.
