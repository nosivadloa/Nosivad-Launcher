Legends of Aria: EternalNosivad shard details

This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Adventurer Levels

Nosivad adds a shard progression layer on top of normal LoA skills and stats.

Visibility

You can see your own numeric Adventurer Level and XP progress. Other players see only your public Adventurer Grade.

GradeMinimum LevelMeaning
E1New adventurer.
D15Early dungeon-ready.
C30Established rift hunter.
B50Danger-zone veteran.
A75High-end rift contender.
S95Peak adventurer status.
Keep the two Grade systems distinct. Adventurer Grade is permanent character progression that controls graded equipment and relative kill XP. Planar Mark Grade controls exchange offers for planar goods; marks are never required for Rift Breach entry. Use /grades or /sigils in game for the combined guide.

XP Sources

Adventurer XP comes from Nosivad-controlled kill hooks: starter mobs, dynamic enemies, planar rift enemies and bosses, corrupted creatures, and world bosses. Bosses and harder content award more XP.

The current maximum Adventurer Level is 100. For skill caps, stat caps, Focus, mana, and Wisdom, see Skills, Stats and Mana.
