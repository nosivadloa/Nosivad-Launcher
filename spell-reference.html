Affliction

Shoots a wisp at the target, stacking them with the debuff Affliction (maximum of 3 stacks). Afflicted targets will take increased Echo damage. Requires Sorcery: 0.0

Consumes a wisp and projects an echo beam in the direction you are facing dealing Echo damage. Enemies that stand in the beam take increased damage the longer they are in it. Your movement speed is greatly reduced. Requires Sorcery: 90.0

Echofall

Consumes a wisp to summon a ball of echo energy that falls at the targeted location dealing 25-50 Echo damage, based on your sorcery skill, within a 3 meter radius. Requires Sorcery: 50.0

Consumes 2 wisps to summon a Construct to fight alongside you for 40 seconds. Only 2 constructs can be summoned at any given time. Requires Sorcery: 80.0

Consumes 1 wisp to summon a Chasers to fight alongside you for 20 seconds. Only four foxes can be summoned at any given time. Requires Sorcery: 20.0

Consume all available wisps, giving you a shield that absorbs 35 damage for each wisp. Shields can prevent spell interrupt but are immediately consumed. Minimum Sorcery: 30.0

Bard Songs (9)

Riddle of Docility

Incapacitates up to 2 nearby hostile creatures, the closest first, for 6 seconds; damage ends the effect. Bosses and creatures that cannot be soothed are immune; it never affects players. Peacemaking always calms a creature held this way.

Riddle of Force

Nearby hostile creatures take increased physical damage for 10 seconds, also from each other when provoked. Increase: 0.3% per Entertainment skill (30% at 100 skill).

Riddle of Havoc

Nearby hostile creatures take increased magic damage for 10 seconds, also from each other when provoked. Increase: 0.3% per Entertainment skill (30% at 100 skill).

Riddle of Indulgence

Lowers your chosen target's Wisdom and its attack speed by 15% for 15 seconds. Lower Wisdom reduces its chance to halve incoming magic damage. Wisdom reduction: 1 per 4 Entertainment skill, rounded up (25 at 100 skill); the attack speed reduction scales the same way.

Riddle of Stupidity

Lowers your chosen target's Will for 15 seconds, reducing its resistance to crowd control. Will reduction: Entertainment skill / 4 (25 at 100 skill). A creature under this riddle is also 10 easier to calm or provoke.

Riddle of Virility

Reduces healing received by your chosen target for 15 seconds. Reduction: 0.3% per Entertainment skill (30% at 100 skill).

Song of Earth

Base stamina regeneration bonus for nearby allies while performing: 0.08 per Entertainment skill per second (8 at 100 skill). Instrument and rune modifiers apply.

Song of Thew

Base health regeneration bonus for nearby allies while performing: Entertainment skill / 5 per second (20 at 100 skill). Instrument and rune modifiers apply.

Song of Water

Base mana regeneration while performing: 0.03 per Entertainment skill per second for nearby allies; 0.01 for you (3 and 1 at 100 skill). Instrument and rune modifiers apply. Hostile creatures and players nearby move 25% slower while the song plays.
