This guide includes base-game information where relevant and current Nosivad rules for this shard. If a Nosivad change affects the base game, follow the Nosivad rule described here.

Adventurer NPCs and Companions

Traveling Adventurers use regional roads, evaluate danger and equipment according to class, and can be hired as short-term companions.

Where They Travel

Adventurers move through fourteen hubs: Eldeir Village, Trinit, Helm, Valus, Belhaven, Pyros Landing, Barren Oasis, Black Forest Treehouse, Asper, Catacombs Vigil, Contempt Front, Corruption Quarantine, Deception Watch, and Ruin Campaign. They follow local road segments, pause at authored points of interest, reverse at route ends, and transfer through regional boundaries for longer journeys.

Classes and Behavior

ClassTypical Behavior
VanguardCloses distance and protects pressured allies.
RangerUses ranged pressure, kites, and swaps to a sidearm at close range.
ArcanistMaintains casting distance, phase-shifts from melee, and can arc damage into nearby hostiles.
ShadowbladeFavors daggers and wounded targets, with stronger finishers below 35% health.
TemplarProtects threatened allies and prioritizes defensive support and healing.

Free Adventurers compare their grade and personality with hostile threat before committing. Their class also influences which enchanted equipment they consider an upgrade.

Hiring a Companion

An initial contract lasts 30 minutes and uses level-scaled pricing. During the contract, the owner can:

  • choose Follow or establish a persistent Guard position;
  • select Defensive, Balanced, or Aggressive tactics;
  • inspect, renew, or dismiss the companion.

Each completed renewal adds another 30 minutes and reduces the next renewal price by 10%, up to a 30% loyalty discount. Unused contract time is preserved when renewing.

Disconnects and Recovery

If an owner disappears or disconnects, the companion waits through a five-minute rendezvous period instead of immediately ending the contract. Returning in time resumes service. Orders, tactics, renewal count, guard position, and remaining contract time follow the companion through its replacement or resurrection path.

Hiring and Recovery Follow-ups

Hired companions clear stale autonomous behavior, follow and catch up to their employer, and assist against the employer's hostile target. Failed road recovery backs off independently for each NPC rather than repeatedly retrying every few seconds.
