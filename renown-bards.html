Town Renown and Bards

Regional leaders remember service performed for their communities. Renown unlocks practical rewards and permanent capstone benefits, while local bards make inns useful places to recover.

Quick check: Use /renown anywhere, or click Renown on the /hud bar, to see all 15 standings, the next threshold, the next reward, and any earned permanent benefit.

How Renown Works

  • Renown is tracked separately for every town, region, and expedition.
  • Speak with that location's leader and choose Open Renown Ledger to accept commissions or report completed service.
  • A qualifying completed task can be recorded once even if it was finished before the renown system or before opening that leader's ledger.
  • Ranks advance at 0, 25, 60, 110, and 180 renown.
  • Ranks two through five grant useful themed items and coins. Highest standing also grants a permanent passive benefit.
  • The global /renown ledger is read-only; rewards are issued by the appropriate regional leader.

Inn Bards and Vitality

Seven inns have dedicated bards in Eldeir, Helm, Valus, Pyros Landing, Barren Oasis, Black Forest, and Asper. When a living player approaches and a hearth is nearby, the bard begins playing. Nearby listeners use the game's normal hearth-music vitality recovery mechanic. The bard stops when no eligible listeners remain and never steals a listener from a real player-led performance.

Pip Merrymarch

Pip is Nosivad's flamboyant wandering bard. He carries a lute, flute, and drum and changes instrument every two minutes. His six-minute repertoire cycles through The Laughing Road, Silverwind Over Eldeir, and The Many-Road March, with a distinct instrument arrangement for each.

Each tune grants a different 30-minute signature buff with the same 60-point primary-stat budget. Pip's Preposterous Panache grants +10 to all six primary stats. Pip's Silverwind Serenade grants +20 Agility, Intelligence, and Wisdom. Pip's Many-Road March grants +20 Strength, Constitution, and Will. These effects replace one another and cannot stack. Choose Hear Pip Perform from his interaction menu to prompt a performance when he is available.
