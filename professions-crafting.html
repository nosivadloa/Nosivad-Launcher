Professions, Crafting and Enchanting

Nosivad keeps the native crafting foundation while adding clearer profession progression, practical construction, item enchanting, and skill-based equipment repair.

Use /professions to review all paths, your earned rank, missing requirements, trainer guidance, and available Start or Resume actions. Use /economy for merchant, recycler, wallet, and service-station guidance.

Profession Paths and Ranks

Fourteen established profession paths use Apprentice, Journeyman, Master, and Grandmaster tiers at 30, 50, 80, and 100 skill. The newer Necromancer combat profession currently offers its first two tiers; see the Necromancy guide for its requirements.

Enchanting Equipment

  1. Use an enchanting table and select a carried weapon, armor piece, shield, or eligible crafted cloak.
  2. Select up to three compatible essence stacks from your backpack.
  3. Review capacity, difficulty, actual success chance, and whether the attempt can improve Enchanting.
  4. Confirm the attempt. Items and essences remain in your backpack until the final transaction is revalidated.
  • Zero-percent attempts are blocked, and existing enchantment families cannot be duplicated.
  • Success consumes one unit from every selected essence stack and applies the effects.
  • Failure never harms the item. At most one selected essence is lost, with higher tiers carrying greater loss risk.
  • Ordinary crafted, looted, rewarded, and legacy equipment is accepted when its type and capacity are valid. Artifacts and unsupported items remain excluded.

Bow Runes (Enchanting Essences)

Rune of Ishi, Rune of Patience, and Rune of Roving are rune-named essences used to enchant bows; they are not ability runes to equip. Each comes in Damaged, standard, and Pristine grades.

  • Ishi: Increased Range Attack — 1-3% Damaged, 5-6% standard, 8-9% Pristine.
  • Patience: Chance to gain Centershot — 1-2% Damaged, 2-4% standard, 4-8% Pristine.
  • Roving: Increased Hit Chance — 1-3% Damaged, 5-6% standard, 8-9% Pristine.

The current game item catalog lists all nine variants under the Enchanting Resources treasure-chest source. Use a bow at an enchanting table; its preview shows the roll before confirmation.

Enchanting Practice

The same table offers Practice batches of up to 100 attempts, limited by carried Magical Essence and remaining skill-cap room. Each attempt costs one ordinary stackable Magical Essence, takes five seconds, and grants 0.1 Enchanting after the completed batch. The complete batch is atomic: cancellation, range loss, missing resources, low vitality, or a cap conflict prevents partial charges and partial gains.

Repair Gear

Ordinary damaged equipment can be repaired through the matching craft station. Metalsmithing handles metal equipment, Fabrication handles cloth, leather, and compatible wearable items, and Woodsmithing handles wooden equipment. The station shows the required skill, wear-scaled material cost, and full restoration before a qualified crafter confirms the guaranteed repair. See Loot and Relics for planar materials, recycling, and Planar Forge services.

Crafted Cloaks

Tailors can make cloaks from Cloth, Quilted Cloth, or Silk Cloth. Player-crafted cloaks can receive compatible armor essences without becoming armor; material determines their enchantment capacity, and the normal three-effect limit remains.

Vocation Renown

Use /vocation or /tradelevel for the separate level-100 gathering and crafting progression. Every five levels earns one Trade Insight. Both category contribution and level gate your choices; see Vocation Renown for surveys, commissions, costs, and examples.
